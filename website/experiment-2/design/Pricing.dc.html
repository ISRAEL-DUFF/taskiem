Questions about price

Do you charge for each task Taskiem does?

No. You pay a flat price each month, however many times your jobs run.

Is VAT included?

VAT is shown separately, so you always see the price and the VAT as two lines.

What do I get in the 14 free days?

You can set up your jobs and try them on your own business for 14 days before you choose a plan.

How does yearly billing work?

A year costs ten months. You pay once for the year and get the other two months free.

Which plan do I need to pay my staff?

Paying staff after you approve comes with Growing Business and above.
