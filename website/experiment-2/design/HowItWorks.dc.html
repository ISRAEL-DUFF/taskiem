How it works

From “hi” to your first result, in one chat.

This is a whole conversation, start to finish. On the left, what you and Taskiem say. On the right, what is happening.

hi

01

Start with hello

No forms and no set-up screens. Open WhatsApp, say hi, and Taskiem answers.

every Friday at 10am text the customers in my debtors table who still owe me

02

Say it your way

Write the job the way you would tell a person. You do not need special words.

Here is what I will do every Friday at 10:00:

  1. Read your Debtors table.
  2. Text each person with a balance above ₦0: “Good morning Chidi, your balance with Ada's Kitchen is ₦12,500. Thank you.”
  3. Send you a count of who was reminded.

Shall I set it up? Reply yes.

yes

03

It reads back, you say yes

Taskiem lists every step and who it will message. Nothing happens until you reply yes.

04

05

It runs without you

Friday comes, the job runs, and you get a short report. Figures here are an example.

Approvals with a PIN

Money always waits for you.

Messages can go out on their own once you have said yes. Payments are different. Each one stops and asks for your PIN first.

  • You see who is paid and how much, before anything moves.
  • Only your PIN approves it. Taskiem cannot approve for you.
  • A payment is sent once, even if the network drops.

The team and their roles

Give your team a part. Keep the PIN.

Add the people who help you run the business and choose what each can do. Roles below are an example.

Owner

Approves payments with a PIN and sees everything.

Manager

Sets up jobs and prepares payments. Cannot approve them.

Cashier

Adds sales and sees the lists you share with them.

Accountant

Reads records and exports them. Changes nothing.
