About Taskiem

Built for how Nigerian businesses already work.

In the chat, with money that has to move carefully, and with no time to learn complicated software. Taskiem is one system that runs the jobs a business does every day.

taskiem · said task-yem

Why

Business already happens in the chat.

Orders come in on WhatsApp. Customers pay by transfer and send a screenshot. Staff ask for money in a voice note. Then someone copies it all into a notebook or a spreadsheet, chases the people who owe, and checks the bank app to see who really paid.

Taskiem does that work. Say what you want done, in your own words, and it becomes a workflow: it takes the order, checks the payment, moves the stock, sends the receipt, reminds the debtor, pays the staff after you approve, and tells you how the day went.

Money is where mistakes hurt most. So in Taskiem, nothing moves without your yes, and nothing is paid twice.

What we hold to

Six things we don't bend on.

  • Money moves onceEvery payment is made exactly once, even when the network fails halfway.
  • You say yesMoney waits for your approval, and staff work within the limits you set.
  • Private by defaultCustomers' details are sealed, and the AI never sees them.
  • Chat first, web tooEverything starts from a message. The web and the app are there when you want a bigger screen.
  • Plain wordsNo triggers, branches or jargon to learn. Taskiem reads back what it will do before it does it.
  • Only what's trueThis site describes what Taskiem does today, not what it might do one day.
