Approvals

The right people say yes, in the right order.

An approval can name who decides and how many must agree, or follow a policy that picks the approvers by the amount. Levels go in order, and a request that waits too long is passed up.

  • Nobody approves their own request, and one person decides only one level.
  • Each yes can ask for your PIN, a passkey or an authenticator code.
  • Publishing a workflow can need a second person too.

Privacy

Customers' details stay sealed.

Phone numbers, emails, BVNs, NINs, account and card numbers are found by themselves and sealed under each person's own key. They're hidden in logs and history, and the AI never sees them. Showing one is a choice that is recorded.

  • Forgetting a customer destroys their key, and needs a passkey or code.
  • Each business's records are kept apart from every other's.
