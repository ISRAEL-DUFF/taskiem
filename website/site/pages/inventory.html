Reordering

Low stock, reordered in one tap.

Set a reorder level for each item. When it runs low, Order what is low drafts one order per supplier. Approve it, and your supplier gets it on WhatsApp to confirm. When the goods arrive, the count goes up and the cost is set, and Taskiem keeps track of what you still owe.

  • Orders above your limit wait for a second person.
  • A supplier on Taskiem gets it straight into their own orders, with your credit terms.

Materials

Count what you use, not only what you sell.

  • A bottle and a pack of six can share one count.
