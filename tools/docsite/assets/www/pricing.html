all_inclusive Are there extra charges if my automated task runs 100 times a day?

expand_more
Never. Taskiem is strictly flat-rate. You pay your fixed monthly subscription in Naira, and your automated workflows can run as frequently as needed—whether that is 5 times or 50,000 times during a promo season. No overage invoices.

pin How does the owner approval PIN work for payments?

expand_more
When an automated rule triggers a money movement (such as a supplier payout, refund, or driver diesel disbursement), Taskiem immediately halts execution and sends an encrypted WhatsApp prompt to the business owner’s registered number. Nothing leaves your bank or wallet until you explicitly enter your private PIN.

payments Can I pay with local cards or direct bank transfer?

expand_more
Yes. We natively support all Nigerian bank cards (Mastercard, Visa, Verve) via Paystack, instant NIP transfers to a dedicated corporate virtual account, and formal corporate invoicing with Nigerian VAT receipts for audited enterprise accounts.

wifi_off What if the internet or GSM network is unstable?

expand_more
Taskiem has built-in idempotency safeguards and message queue persistence. If network connectivity drops while sending an alert or processing a Moniepoint/OPay webhook, our engine cryptographically verifies state before retrying—guaranteeing that you never double-pay suppliers or duplicate debtor notifications.
