The fine print, in plain words

Questions about paying.

What happens when I go over?

WhatsApp messages beyond your plan are charged at Meta’s rate, passed through at cost and shown on your invoice. You can move up a plan at any time.

Can I pay yearly?

Yes. A year costs ten months’ price.

What if I stop paying?

You get {{if .Pricing.GraceDays}}{{.Pricing.GraceDays}} days’{{else}}a period of{{end}} grace. After that new runs pause until you pay. Nothing is deleted during your plan’s retention period.

Do you charge for failed runs?

No. You never pay per run, failed or not.

How do I pay?

By card or bank transfer. Invoices come as numbered PDFs with VAT{{if .Pricing.VATPercent}} at {{.Pricing.VATPercent}}%{{end}}, to your email and your chat.
