About

Made in Lagos, for businesses that run on WhatsApp.

Taskiem is made in Nigeria by the team behind Xenibase, a managed database used by Nigerian developers.

We built Taskiem because every small business owner we know runs their business from WhatsApp and a notebook, and the tools built for them were built for someone else: for people with laptops, email and an hour to learn a dashboard.

So Taskiem starts in the chat you already have open. It speaks plainly, tells you what it will do before it does it, and treats your customers’ details and your money with the care a bank would expect. The same engine serves lenders and payroll companies, which is why a shop gets protections that are usually reserved for them.

Taskiem bills in naira and connects to the services businesses here already use.
