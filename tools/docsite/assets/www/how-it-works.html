What a workflow is

Something Taskiem does for you again and again: on a schedule, or when something happens, such as a payment arriving or a row being added to your table. Each one is a few plain steps you can read, change, pause, and see every time it ran.

{{template "i-screen"}}

On the web too

Everything you can do in chat you can do in the web app: the same workflows as a list of steps, your tables as a grid, your team and their permissions, every run and what it did.

{{template "i-voice"}}

In your language

Taskiem speaks English today, and understands voice notes (in beta). Nigerian Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa and Igbo are being reviewed by native speakers and will be switched on when they are right.

{{template "i-users"}}

Your team

Add your accountant, your manager or your sales staff. Your accountant can see payments but not approve them; your manager can approve up to a limit you set. Everyone’s actions are on record under their own name.

{{template "i-book"}}

Every run on record

See each time a workflow ran, step by step: who it messaged, what it paid, and what went wrong if something did.

{{template "i-lock"}}

Your keys stay sealed

You connect a service once. Taskiem stores its key encrypted and never shows it again, to anyone.
