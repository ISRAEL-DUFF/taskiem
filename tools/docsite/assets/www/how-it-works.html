What a workflow is

A workflow is something Taskiem does for you again and again: on a schedule, or when something happens, such as a payment arriving or a row being added to your table. Each one is a few plain steps. You can read them, change them, pause them, and see every time they ran.

On the web too

Everything you can do in chat you can do in the web app: the same workflows as a list of steps, your tables as a grid, your team and their permissions, every run and what it did. Use whichever is in your hand.

In your language

Taskiem speaks English today, and understands voice notes (in beta). Nigerian Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa and Igbo are being reviewed by native speakers and will be switched on when they are right.

Approvals

Money waits for your yes

Salaries, supplier payments and transfers stop and ask. The request comes to you on WhatsApp or Telegram with buttons; you approve or reject with one tap, and a PIN where you set one. Taskiem then pays each person once, texts them, and tells you when it is done.

Your team

Add your accountant, your manager or your sales staff. Each gets their own role: your accountant can see payments but not approve them; your manager can approve up to a limit you set. Everyone's actions are on record under their own name.
