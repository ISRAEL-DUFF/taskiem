# 0061 — Business links: two businesses on Taskiem trade as mirrored records under each one's own tenancy

Date: 2026-10-30 · Status: **Accepted** (built 30 October to 2 November 2026) · Plan: [business links](../spec/business-links.md)

## Context

Every counterparty of a business is an outsider to Taskiem: a customer is a sealed name and phone on an invoice, a supplier a name and a phone reached on WhatsApp, and the documents between businesses travel as messages with buttons and links ([0039](0039-reaching-people-outside.md), [0045](0045-tally-in-taskiem.md), [0057](0057-stock-by-chat.md)). When both businesses are on Taskiem, each side retypes what the other recorded: a purchase order arrives as a chat message, an invoice as an SMS link, a payment as money from a phone number to be matched by hand.

The founder named the gap: "these business associates don't have a way to sync their business data, such as invoices, orders, etc. They don't have a way to auto order, inquire, pay or part-pay from within Taskiem." Three choices were settled with him: invoices and payments first, then orders, then price lists, with everything included; businesses link both by looking each other up and asking, and by being offered the link at their first document; each business keeps its own copy of every document, with one side's copy the truth for each fact, rather than a shared document.

Tenancy already has a pattern for touching another tenant with consent: a partner enters a sub-tenant through one definer that checks the relationship, audits in both tenants and narrows the scope for the transaction ([0015](0015-embedding-tenancy.md)). Matching already pays a customer's oldest invoice exactly from their own registered account number ([0046](0046-balans-in-taskiem.md)).

## Decision

1. **A link is a consented relationship between two businesses.** One requests, the other accepts; either ends it. Nothing crosses before acceptance, and a lookup reveals nothing before it: a name or what-is-sold search lists only businesses whose findability is on (on by default where a storefront is published, since that content is already public; off and asked once otherwise), and a blind lookup by phone or address hears nothing but an acceptance, so decline, a block and no business at all are the same silence.
2. **Documents cross as mirrors, each side under its own tenancy.** A linked supplier's invoice is a bill in the buyer's business; a buyer's purchase order is an order in the seller's; statuses, payments, amendments and thread messages are mirrored. Each business owns its copy; a mirror names its source by the link and the source's reference, and every mirrored field has exactly one source: the issuer's invoice for what is owed, the seller's match for what is paid (`paid_kobo`), the seller's order for accepted and delivered. What a side says for itself (`declared_paid_kobo`, the buyer's `received` confirmation) is its own field beside the mirrored one, never overwritten; the two disagreeing is a dispute on the document, not drift for the reconciler.
3. **Three definers, and nothing else, cross between businesses.** Every cross-business write is one call to `taskiem_link_enter` (the link active, the caller a side, an audit entry in both businesses, the scope narrowed for that transaction) and the writes for that one event into the other side's own tables. All three are owned by `taskiem_dispatch`, like every definer; EXECUTE is granted to `taskiem_app`, which the api, worker and scheduler processes use, and not to `taskiem_public`, which the public host and mail role use. Pairing a payout with a credit is two transactions, one per side, joined by a pairing token Taskiem writes into the payout's narration, since the two rails issue their own references; where the rail does not pass the narration on, a third read-only definer answers the one unpaired payout's token by amount and time, or nothing, and a payout unpaired after three days raises an alert. The token is found before the credit is matched, so the match goes to the invoice the payout named. The reconciler reads through a read-only definer, `taskiem_link_digests`, which returns per-document digests from both sides and no content, and rewrites a mirror through an ordinary mirror write. No session, key or member ever holds the other business in scope.
4. **The link moves no money.** A buyer pays through its own connection's payout path, under its own approval policy and "First payment to this account"; the seller's credit is matched by the customer account number issued to the buyer at acceptance, exactly: to the invoice the payment names when it names one (a payout for a chosen bill, or a narration with the reference), else oldest invoice first. The link pairs the payout and the credit by the pairing token, so one transfer is paid once and matched twice, and neither side's To match sees it. Paying the invoice a payment names, and skipping an invoice whose bill is disputed, are changes to matching made in L1 for customers keyed by a link.
5. **A purchase order to a linked supplier is an order, not a message.** It starts the seller's order workflow like any order, accepted automatically where the seller set that for the link, and its life flows back to the purchase order; the invoice it raises is the buyer's bill.
6. **Counterparties not on Taskiem are unchanged.** Invoices, purchase orders and updates reach them as today. A link changes only the documents between two linked businesses.
7. **Price lists, threads, statements, credit terms and reminders ride on the same link**, each a mirror or a setting, never a shared record. Workflow triggers and actions for links (`link@1`) carry references and amounts, never the other side's contact details.
8. **"Partner" keeps its meaning.** A platform partner ([0015](0015-embedding-tenancy.md)) serves sub-tenants through the partner API; a linked business is a peer. The owner-facing word is "linked business".
9. **Linked businesses talk in a conversation that belongs to the pair.** On the web it is WhatsApp's look, feel and way of working, deliberately and in detail (the layout, the bubbles, the ticks, quoted replies, voice notes, search, mute and archive, and the words for them), with only Taskiem's colours and type: almost every business already knows WhatsApp, and a chat that works differently would defeat the purpose. One conversation per linked business with its whole history, every document a card in the stream; on the phone it is the WhatsApp or Telegram the owner uses, the same messages with the business's name. The web app becomes a channel on the one conversation engine, with the owner's own Taskiem conversation in the same list. Writing to a linked business needs `chat.trade`; a conversation is muted or archived, never deleted by one side.
10. **A mention is a structured reference.** `@` resolves a linked business and `#` an item on its price list at the moment of typing, so one message can read back as one draft per business and one **Yes** sends them all; a mention of an unlinked business offers the link.
11. **Businesses are found from the Chat page's search, as WhatsApp's finds contacts.** The search box above the conversation list gives chats, messages and businesses on Taskiem to link with, by name, by what they sell or do, and by area, from an index of published storefront content and the card of businesses whose findability is on; a phone or address query reveals nothing. No search in the app's header, and no search over the business's own records in this decision. It is a search, not a directory: nothing to browse, no ratings, no promoted listings.

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| A shared document both businesses edit, in a shared tenant or a neutral one | Breaks the tenancy model (every row has one tenant, one audit chain, one key); every reader of either side would need a third scope; a dispute would be an edit war. Mirrors with a named truth per fact keep isolation and still agree |
| Keep messages, add buttons ("Record as a bill") | Still retyping, with each side's record drifting; no pairing of payments; no automation |
| Links through the partner API, one business as the other's partner | A partner holds its sub-tenants' plans and limits and enters them at will; peers trade as equals and each keeps its own plan, people and consent |
| Settlement through Taskiem (Taskiem takes the money and pays it on) | Taskiem is not a licensed intermediary for that; the rails are. An internal transfer on Taskiem's own rail is named for later, through the rail, not through Taskiem |
| Discovery by phone that says whether a business exists | Lets anyone learn who is on Taskiem by typing numbers; the lookup answers the same words either way and only acceptance reveals |
| An app-native chat panel in Taskiem's own style | Every owner already knows WhatsApp; anything that works differently has to be learned, and the chat's point is that nothing has to be |
| Threads on documents instead of a conversation | A trade is a relationship, not a set of tickets; owners already run it in WhatsApp threads with whole histories, and a messenger with documents as cards is what they would recognise |
| A separate chat product beside the conversation engine | Two engines for the same words; the web channel on `converse` gives the web every command chat has, and the phone every message the web has |

## Consequences

As built (migrations 00279 to 00286 and 00291; the plan's §16.1 to §16.8):

- **Tables.** Platform-level, read by no business directly: `business_links` (read through `taskiem_links_of`), `link_blind_requests`, `link_typing`. Tenant-owned under forced row-level security: `link_settings` (the link code, findability), `link_offers`; `bills` (`open`, `paying`, `part_paid`, `paid`, `disputed`, `void`; `declared_paid_kobo`, `paying_kobo` and `sent_kobo` beside the mirrored `paid_kobo`), `bill_payments` (the pairing token, `paired_at`), `bill_pay_settings`, `link_notices`, `link_mirrors_due`; `link_orders` beside `storefront_orders` of the kind `trade`, `trade_settings` (auto-accept, invoice at acceptance or delivery, `terms_days`, `credit_limit_kobo`), `trade_item_links`, `link_orders_due`, `suppliers.link_id` and `deliver_to`, the purchase order's seller-side columns and `over_limit_at` on the order; `link_messages` and `link_conversations` (a thread is a quoted reply, not a store), `link_chat_sent`, `chat_history` (the Taskiem conversation's history, 90 days); `price_lists`, `price_list_items`, `price_list_shares`, `price_list_overrides`, the buyer's mirrors `supplier_price_lists`, `supplier_prices` and `supplier_terms`, and `item_links`; `link_starts` (the link trigger's queued starts, 90 days). Invoices and customer records carry the key `link:<tenant>`, and `customer_invoices.disputed`.
- **Definers**, all owned by `taskiem_dispatch`, executable by `taskiem_app` only and listed in `definers.tsv`: the one door, `taskiem_link_enter` (link active, caller a side, both audit chains, the scope narrowed for the rest of the transaction), modelled on `taskiem_partner_enter`; the reads `taskiem_links_of`, `taskiem_link_search`, `taskiem_link_by_code`, `taskiem_link_offer_to`; `taskiem_link_request` and `taskiem_link_answer`, which write the platform table; the workers' claims `taskiem_link_mirrors_claim` and `taskiem_link_orders_claim`; the reconciler `taskiem_link_reconcile` (in place of the planned `taskiem_link_digests`); `taskiem_link_payout_token`, called by the matcher once per credit; the typing line's `taskiem_link_typing` and `taskiem_link_typing_of`; the purges `taskiem_chat_history_purge` and `taskiem_link_starts_purge`; and `taskiem_public_link_code` for the public role, which prints a link code on an invoice's page and nothing else. The isolation harness (`api/links_isolation_test.go`) allows the link functions and no other query naming the other business. `taskiem_money_leak_tenants` also lists businesses with open bills, so the morning summary reaches a buyer-only business.
- **Permissions.** `link.manage` (owners and admins) asks, accepts, blocks and ends links and sets findability; `chat.trade` (owners, admins and operators) writes in conversations, and a workflow's `reply` needs it of the person who published the workflow. Plan limits for links, requests and mirrored documents are counted as other limits are, against the sender.
- **Pages and words.** Chat (the conversation list with its search box, the conversation, the Taskiem conversation), Linked businesses, Bills and a bill's page, Trade orders, Price lists, the link's Statement, **On Taskiem** and **Prices** on a supplier's row, a link code on the QR card, invoices and receipts; in chat `link`, `links`, `find …`, `who sells …`, `find us`, `hide us`, `bills`, `bill INV-0042`, `bills due`, `prices from …`, `order … from …`, `ask … (about …)`, `@` and `#` mentions, and **Pay** on a bill's card and its reminder (paying from chat is a button, not a word).
- **Automation.** The `link` trigger (`bill.received`, `bill.paid`, `order.received`, `order.delivered`, `thread.message`, `link.accepted`), `link@1` (`get_bill`, `list_bills`, `pay_bill`, `get_order`, `order_step`, `price_of`, `low_orders`, `place_order`, `reply`; writes in `prod` only), the templates `pay-bills-on-approval`, `reorder-from-linked-suppliers`, `tell-me-when-a-linked-customer-orders` and `linked-order-to-delivery`, the managed `pay-linked-bill` behind Pay, and the builder's rule and context.
- **Money and matching.** A payout for a bill carries the pairing token in its narration and is paired with the seller's credit by it; matching's exact match on a link account pays the invoice a payment names, else the oldest open invoice, and passes over an invoice whose bill is queried (`docs/matching.md`). A purchase order to a linked supplier is cancelled freely before acceptance and by a request after it (`docs/stock.md`).
- **Docs.** Invoices, stock, storefronts, matching, the owners' guide ("Trade with businesses on Taskiem"), the atlas (business modules, identity and tenancy, security, data model, triggers and channels, connectors, overview) and the threat model (the actor "a linked business").

## As built

Built in eight milestones on one branch (the plan's §16.1 to §16.8 say what each became). Where the build differs from the decision above:

- **The mirror is queued, not written in the sender's transaction** (decision 3). A trigger notes every change to a sent linked invoice in the seller's `link_mirrors_due`, and the link worker writes the bill in a transaction of its own, through `taskiem_link_enter`, with back-off; a purchase order to a linked supplier, each change to the seller's order, a price list and a credit check go the same way through `link_orders_due`. The seller's document is never refused for the buyer's side, and every path that changes a document is covered without touching each one. A conversation message and a read are still written in the sender's transaction.
- **The reconciler is `taskiem_link_reconcile()`, not `taskiem_link_digests`** (decision 3). One definer compares every active link's invoices, sent since the link was accepted, with their bills by the mirrored figures (number, total, paid, due date, void), hourly, and puts each that differs or is missing back on its seller's mirror list, answering a count only; the worker writes it again from the invoice. Lines are not compared, and no content crosses.
- **The pairing token** (decision 3 and 4) is the bill's reference and a code (`INV-0042/7QX4`) on the buyer's `bill_payments` row, written into the payout's narration; `taskiem_link_payout_token` answers the token of the buyer's one unpaired payout of an amount in three days, or nothing, called by the matcher once per credit, and the buyer's payment is marked paired (`paired_at`) through the door as the match's last write. The alert for a payout unpaired after three days is not built.
- **More than three definers cross between businesses** (decision 3). Besides the door, the reconciler and the payout token, the link itself (reads, requests, answers), the workers' claims, the typing line and the purges are definers too, each owned by `taskiem_dispatch`, granted to `taskiem_app` alone and listed in `definers.tsv`; the public role has one, which prints an invoice page's link code. The isolation test allows the link functions and nothing else; what matters is held: no session, key or member ever holds the other business in scope.
- **Over the limit, an order is flagged, not held** (decision 7). It reaches the seller marked `over_limit_at` and is not accepted by itself; the seller may accept it anyway, which clears the flag; the buyer is told why it waits and offered its bills; a run may not accept it.
- **Reminders** reach a linked buyer as a card on the bill and a notice in its chat and bell, with **Pay**; a queried bill is not reminded, and its invoice is left out of the overdue lists until the query is settled.
- **The link trigger is its own trigger type** (`link`), not a `connector_event` of `link@1`: the events are Taskiem's own and need no connection. `link@1` acts for the run's own business as the system, reads in any environment, writes in `prod` only, and writes in a conversation only when the person who published the workflow holds `chat.trade`.
- **The approval before a bill is paid is the bill payments' policy's**: `pay_bill` and **Pay** start the managed `pay-linked-bill` workflow, whose approver approves it, so the pay template has no approval step of its own; the reorder template approves the morning's orders as one list before any is sent, which the plan did not say.
- **Price lists are built on the seller's stock items**, not on a front's list block: fronts were not on the branch. **The statement is computed** from open invoices and bills, nothing stored. **Mentions are of businesses only.**
- **The Taskiem conversation on the web keeps its history** (`chat_history`, 90 days, sealed, deleted by a purge), a product decision that replaced the platform's earlier rule of keeping none of the conversation.
- **Not built:** "Is this invoice for <business>?" when an invoice goes to a linked business's owner's phone (no invoice becomes a bill by a phone alone, as decided); the bill's thread opening by itself after seven days of a declared payment; auto-pay per link; the alert on a payout unpaired after three days; a `paused` link; the business's kind and area in the search for a business with no storefront, which is found by name only.
