# User stories: what businesses get from the finished product

Date: 2026-10-16 · Status: a catalogue for product planning

This page imagines Taskiem and Xenibase fully built and on production in Nigeria, and lists what businesses and the people around them can then do. Each story is written from the person's side. The companion page is [the chat-first strategy](chat-first-nigeria.md), which says what is built today and what the order of the rest should be. A story here is a target, not a claim: where today's code already does it, the strategy page says so.

Stories are grouped by who benefits. Numbers are for reference in planning, not priority.

## A. The first five minutes: getting started from a phone

1. As a shop owner, I can save a number and send "hi" on WhatsApp, and get a business account made for me from the chat, with no website, email or app to install.
2. As a new user, I can answer three questions in chat (my business name, what I sell, how customers pay me) and be offered the three automations most like mine, in plain words.
3. As a new user, I can say what I want in my own words ("every evening tell me how much I sold") and have it built, read back as numbered steps, and running within one conversation.
4. As a new user, I can connect my Paystack, Moniepoint or OPay account by tapping a form in WhatsApp and pasting a key, never leaving the chat.
5. As a new user, I can connect my Google account by tapping a link in WhatsApp and granting access, then return to the chat where it says "connected".
6. As a new user, I can be told exactly what the automation will do, who it will message and what it will never do, and approve it with one **yes**.
7. As a new user, I can see my first automation run for real within fifteen minutes of my first message, and get the result in the same chat.
8. As a new user, I can speak a voice note instead of typing, in English, Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa or Igbo, and be read back what was heard before anything happens.
9. As a new user, I can choose to be spoken to in my language, and switch at any time with one word.
10. As a feature-phone user, I can reach the same automations through a USSD code, with menus that fit my screen, and get outcomes by SMS.
11. As a new user, I can invite my accountant or my manager by sending their WhatsApp number, and they get their own role and their own limits.
12. As a new user, I can try everything for a fortnight at no cost, and pay by card or bank transfer in naira when I decide to stay.
13. As a new user, I can ask "what can you do?" and get a short list that fits my business, not a manual.
14. As a new user, I can stop at any point with "cancel" and nothing will have run or been charged.

## B. The everyday owner: money in

15. As a trader, I can be texted every time money enters my account, with who paid and how much, and have it logged in my sales ledger automatically.
16. As a trader, I can have every customer thanked by SMS or WhatsApp the moment Paystack confirms their payment, with a receipt they can keep.
17. As a trader, I can get a 6 pm message with today's sales total, the number of customers and the best-selling item.
18. As a trader, I can get a Monday morning summary of last week against the week before.
19. As a trader, I can ask "how much did I make today?" at any time and get the figure from my records.
20. As a trader, I can be told when a payment fails or is reversed, with the customer's name, so I do not release goods against money that never came.
21. As a trader, I can be warned when my Paystack or Moniepoint balance drops below a level I set.
22. As a trader, I can have each day's takings swept to my savings account at closing time, after I approve the amount with a PIN.
23. As a trader, I can give customers a payment link in chat, and be told when it is paid.
24. As a trader, I can have transfers that arrive with no reference matched to open orders by amount and time, and be asked only about the ones that do not match.
25. As a trader, I can get a month-end statement of every payment in and out, as a PDF in WhatsApp and a sheet in my email.

## C. The everyday owner: money owed to me

26. As a supplier, I can keep a list of customers who buy on credit by sending their name, number and amount in chat, and have it kept for me in a proper record, not a sheet I must maintain.
27. As a supplier, I can have every debtor reminded every Friday at 10 am of what they owe, politely, in the language they speak.
28. As a supplier, I can have reminders escalate: a gentle message at seven days, a firmer one at fourteen, and a message to me at twenty-one.
29. As a supplier, I can have a debt marked paid automatically when a transfer for that amount arrives from that customer, and the reminders stop on their own.
30. As a supplier, I can ask "who owes me?" and get the list, sorted by amount and age.
31. As a supplier, I can tell the agent "Chidi paid 20,000 cash" and have the record updated.
32. As a supplier, I can have an invoice sent after every delivery and followed up on day three, day seven and day fourteen if unpaid.
33. As a supplier, I can offer a customer who owes me a payment link in the reminder, so paying is one tap.
34. As a supplier, I can see which customers always pay late and decide whom to keep on credit.

## D. The everyday owner: money out

35. As an owner, I can pay every person on my staff list on the 25th by bank transfer, after I approve the total with my PIN, and have each one texted that they have been paid.
36. As an owner, I can be reminded three days before payday with the amount I need to have ready.
37. As an owner, I can approve a supplier payment from WhatsApp with one tap, and know that the same payment can never go out twice, whatever fails.
38. As an owner, I can set a rule that any payment above ₦200,000 needs both me and my partner to approve.
39. As an owner, I can have my rent, my generator diesel and my internet paid on their due dates, with a reminder the day before and an approval each time.
40. As an owner, I can have electricity, airtime and data bought on a schedule through a bills connector and logged against the branch that used them.
41. As an owner, I can be told the moment a payout fails or is reversed, who it was to, and what to do.
42. As an owner, I can have my accountant emailed a copy of every payment with its receipt, without being copied on every message myself.
43. As an owner, I can pay a supplier in Ghana or Kenya through mobile money from the same chat.
44. As an owner, I can ask "what did I pay out this month?" and get the breakdown.

## E. Stock, orders and deliveries

45. As a store owner, I can keep my stock list by chat ("add 50 cartons of Indomie at 8,500") and have it kept for me.
46. As a store owner, I can be texted every morning with the items at or below their reorder level.
47. As a store owner, I can have a purchase order drafted to my supplier on WhatsApp when stock runs low, and sent after I approve it.
48. As a store owner, I can have stock reduced automatically when a sale is recorded, and ask "how many bags of rice do I have?" at any time.
49. As an online seller, I can be texted each new order from my shop, with the customer's name, items and address.
50. As an online seller, I can have each customer told when their order is confirmed, packed, dispatched and delivered.
51. As an online seller, I can have a dispatch rider assigned from my rider list and told the address, and be told when the rider confirms delivery.
52. As an online seller, I can have cash-on-delivery orders followed up until the rider's remittance arrives, and be told which riders are short.
53. As an online seller, I can have an order held when the payment has not cleared, and released the moment it does.
54. As an online seller, I can have a customer's complaint about an order routed to me with the order's history attached.
55. As a wholesaler, I can have price lists sent to every retailer on my list when I change them.
56. As a market trader, I can record sales by voice note during the day and have the day's total worked out for me at night.

## F. Customers talking to the business

57. As a business, I can give customers a WhatsApp number of my own where they can check an order, pay a bill or book a slot from a menu, without a person answering.
58. As a business, I can have the menu answer in the customer's language.
59. As a business, I can have a customer confirmed by a code sent to their phone before they see anything about their account.
60. As a business, I can have frequently asked questions answered from my own answers, and anything else handed to a person with the conversation so far.
61. As a business, I can have a customer's message that says "complaint" or "refund" raised to me at once, with a button to approve the refund.
62. As a business, I can let customers book appointments from a menu and be reminded the day before, and have a no-show freed for the next person.
63. As a business, I can run a USSD code for customers who do not have data, with the same menu.
64. As a business, I can collect a customer's details on a WhatsApp form (name, address, size) and have them stored as a customer record.
65. As a business, I can ask every customer for a one-tap rating after delivery and be told when ratings fall.
66. As a business, I can send an approved WhatsApp message to all customers who bought in the last ninety days, and see who replied.
67. As a business, I can have a welcome message, a loyalty reward after five purchases and a win-back message after sixty quiet days, each sent on its own.

## G. Staff, approvals and control

68. As an owner, I can give my manager the right to run automations but not to change them, and my accountant the right to see everything but move nothing.
69. As an owner, I can approve requests from staff with a tap, see exactly who asked and why, and know that nobody can approve their own request.
70. As an owner, I can require my PIN for anything that moves money, and my passkey for anything above a limit.
71. As an owner, I can delegate my approvals to my deputy for the week I travel, and take them back when I return.
72. As an owner, I can be told when a request has waited too long for an approver.
73. As an owner, I can have a staff member's access end the day they leave, by one message.
74. As an owner, I can see a record of everything that happened, who did it and when, that nobody can alter, and give it to my auditor or my bank.
75. As an owner, I can ask "what ran today, what failed, what is waiting for me?" and get the three lists.
76. As an owner, I can be told in one message when something fails, what it was, and what the agent suggests, and accept the fix from the chat.
77. As an owner, I can pause all automations for a day with one message and resume them later, and nothing will be lost in between.
78. As a staff member, I can start an automation I am allowed to run, fill its form in WhatsApp and get its outcome, without knowing it is an automation.
79. As a staff member, I can submit an expense from chat with a photo of the receipt, and be told when it is approved and paid.
80. As a staff member, I can request leave, have it routed to the right approver and be told the answer.

## H. Records without a database

81. As an owner, I can tell the agent "keep a list of my customers" and have a real, backed-up record created for me, which I fill by chat and never see as a database.
82. As an owner, I can add, correct and search records by chat ("change Ngozi's number to 0803…", "who bought from me in June?").
83. As an owner, I can get any record as a sheet in my email or a PDF in WhatsApp at any time.
84. As an owner, I can import the sheet I already keep, once, and never maintain it by hand again.
85. As an owner, I can let my web developer build a shop or an app on the same records through a documented API, with sign-in by phone code and file storage included.
86. As an owner, I can have every record backed up nightly and restored to any point in time if something goes wrong.
87. As an owner, I can ask for a correction to be undone, and have it undone.
88. As an owner, I can have a customer's data erased on request, everywhere it was kept, and get a confirmation to show them.
89. As a growing business, I can move from a shared record to a dedicated database with no change to my automations.

## I. Schools

90. As a school owner, I can have fee invoices sent to every parent at term start, with a payment link, and reminders until paid.
91. As a school owner, I can have fee payments matched to pupils automatically and receipts sent to parents on WhatsApp.
92. As a school owner, I can ask "who has not paid?" by class.
93. As a school owner, I can have parents told when their child is absent, by 9 am, from the register the teacher fills on a form.
94. As a school owner, I can have results released to each parent individually on WhatsApp, with the code sent to the phone on record.
95. As a school owner, I can pay teachers on the 25th after approval, and have PAYE and pension computed and remitted.
96. As a school owner, I can send the term calendar, closures and PTA notices to every parent in their language.
97. As a school owner, I can let parents request a transcript or a letter from a menu and have it sent after a fee is paid.

## J. Churches, mosques and associations

98. As a church administrator, I can have every tithe and offering transfer recorded against the giver and a thank-you sent, with an annual statement each January.
99. As an association secretary, I can collect dues from members by payment link, remind those who have not paid, and send receipts.
100. As an association secretary, I can send meeting notices to all members and collect attendance by one-tap reply.
101. As a cooperative treasurer, I can record members' savings and loans, send monthly balances, and have loan repayments matched when they arrive.
102. As a cooperative treasurer, I can have any payout approved by two signatories before it goes out.
103. As an NGO programme officer, I can disburse stipends to beneficiaries by mobile money after approval, with a verification code first, and report every payment to the donor.

## K. Logistics and transport

104. As a logistics company, I can have every shipment's status sent to the sender and the receiver at each stage.
105. As a logistics company, I can have a proof-of-delivery photo collected from the rider and attached to the shipment.
106. As a logistics company, I can have riders' daily remittances reconciled against deliveries and the shortfalls listed.
107. As a transport company, I can let passengers book and pay by WhatsApp or USSD, and send tickets with a code.
108. As a transport company, I can pay drivers weekly from trip records after approval.
109. As a fleet owner, I can be reminded of each vehicle's licence, insurance and service dates, and have the payments made on approval.

## L. Property and estates

110. As an estate manager, I can have service charges invoiced to every resident, reminded, matched when paid and receipted.
111. As an estate manager, I can let residents report a fault from a menu and have it assigned to the right artisan, with updates to the resident.
112. As a landlord, I can be reminded of rent due dates, have tenants reminded, and be told when rent arrives.
113. As an agent, I can have every enquiry about a listing captured from WhatsApp with the buyer's details, and a follow-up sent two days later.
114. As an estate manager, I can let residents pre-register visitors from a menu and have the gate told.

## M. Clinics, pharmacies and labs

115. As a clinic, I can let patients book from a menu, be reminded the day before, and be followed up after the visit.
116. As a clinic, I can send lab results to the patient's own number after a code, never to anyone else.
117. As a pharmacy, I can remind chronic patients when their refill is due and take the order by chat.
118. As a pharmacy, I can be told each morning which drugs are near expiry or below reorder level.
119. As a clinic, I can bill HMOs monthly from visit records and follow up on unpaid claims.
120. As a clinic, I can have every message to a patient keep their data masked, and be able to show the regulator who saw what.

## N. Hospitality and food

121. As a restaurant, I can take orders from a WhatsApp menu, confirm payment, and tell the kitchen.
122. As a restaurant, I can have the day's sales summed by item and the owner told at closing.
123. As a hotel, I can confirm bookings, send check-in details and request a review after check-out.
124. As a caterer, I can collect a deposit by link when an order is placed and the balance two days before the event.
125. As an event planner, I can collect vendor invoices, route them for approval and pay them on the agreed dates.

## O. Agriculture and agents

126. As an agro-dealer, I can register farmers by voice note and USSD, and send planting and price updates in their language.
127. As an aggregator, I can pay farmers for produce delivered, from weighbridge records, by mobile money after approval.
128. As an input supplier, I can sell on credit to registered farmers and have repayments matched at harvest.
129. As a POS agent network, I can onboard agents by chat, settle their commissions weekly, and tell each agent their balance by USSD.

## P. Fintechs, microfinance banks and regulated businesses

130. As a microfinance bank, I can disburse loans after a maker-checker approval, with every step recorded in a tamper-evident log the regulator can verify.
131. As a microfinance bank, I can have loan repayments collected on their due dates by direct debit or mandate, with retries and a reminder before each one.
132. As a microfinance bank, I can verify an applicant's BVN and NIN and check their bank statements before a loan is approved, all inside the workflow.
133. As a fintech, I can be certain that a transfer whose outcome is unknown is held for a person and never sent again blind.
134. As a fintech, I can have every failed payout investigated automatically, with a classification and a proposed fix, and a person deciding.
135. As a fintech, I can have a provider's API change detected and flagged before it breaks a workflow.
136. As a fintech, I can require passkeys for publishing a workflow that moves money, and a second person for the publish itself.
137. As a fintech, I can keep customers' personal data sealed, masked in every message, and erased on request.
138. As a fintech, I can export a compliance report of approvals, step-ups and payments for any period, and prove the audit log was not altered.
139. As a fintech, I can sign in my team with our identity provider and provision them by SCIM.
140. As a fintech, I can bring my own encryption key, and know that revoking it makes my data unreadable to anyone.
141. As a fintech, I can run my workflows on dedicated workers with fixed outgoing addresses that my providers allow-list.
142. As a fintech, I can keep my workflows in Git, test them in a staging environment with mocked providers, and promote them through a gate.
143. As a fintech, I can write a step in TypeScript or Python when a connector does not do what I need, and have it run in a sandbox that cannot reach anything I did not allow.
144. As a payroll company, I can run each client's payroll as a workflow with their own approvers, and report reversals that come back weeks later.

## Q. SaaS companies and partners

145. As a SaaS company, I can embed the automation builder in my own product, under my brand and domain, so my customers automate without knowing Taskiem exists.
146. As a SaaS company, I can give each of my customers their own isolated space, with their own approvers and limits.
147. As a SaaS company, I can expose my own API as a connector so my customers' workflows can act on my product.
148. As a SaaS company, I can install a workflow package for every new customer, pre-filled, from my own onboarding.
149. As a SaaS company, I can route provider events for many customers through one shared provider account, with each customer's events going only to them.
150. As a bank, I can offer automations to my SME customers from my own WhatsApp number, with payments through my rails.
151. As a telco, I can offer a USSD automation menu to subscribers without smartphones.
152. As a connector publisher, I can build a connector for a Nigerian provider from its public docs, test it against the conformance kit, submit it for review and be listed in the catalogue.

## R. The agent that builds and repairs

153. As an owner, I can describe what I want in English or Pidgin and get an automation drafted from a template that fits, with the details I did not give asked one at a time.
154. As an owner, I can say "change it to Saturdays" or "also email my accountant" and have the existing automation changed, read back and confirmed.
155. As an owner, I can ask "why did this fail?" and get a plain answer and a proposed fix that was tested on the real failed data before I see it.
156. As an owner, I can accept a fix with one tap, and have the failed run resumed from where it stopped without repeating anything that already happened.
157. As an owner, I can be sure that the agent never publishes, approves, reads a secret or moves money on its own, whatever I or anyone else types.
158. As an owner, I can ask the agent to explain any automation in plain words, step by step.
159. As an owner, I can be told before I save that an automation moves money without an approval, and have the approval added for me.
160. As an owner, I can have the agent run an automation on test data first and show me what each step would do.
161. As an owner, I can have the agent suggest an automation when it notices a pattern ("you text the same five people every Friday; shall I do it?").
162. As an owner, I can have an incoming customer message classified by an AI step inside a workflow (complaint, order, question) and routed accordingly.
163. As an owner, I can have an AI step draft a reply to a customer's question from my own answers, and send it only after I approve, or automatically where I allow it.
164. As an owner, I can have a photo of a handwritten receipt or a bank alert read into a record.
165. As an owner, I can have the agent's monthly AI use capped at my plan's allowance and be told before it runs out.

## S. Reliability and trust, as the business sees it

166. As an owner, I can trust that a payment is made exactly once, even if the network, the provider or the platform fails mid-way.
167. As an owner, I can trust that a workflow started by a bank transfer that was later reversed is undone, step by step.
168. As an owner, I can trust that when the platform is down for an hour, events that arrived meanwhile are processed when it returns, in order, none lost.
169. As an owner, I can see a public status page and be told on WhatsApp about incidents that affect me.
170. As an owner, I can know my data is hosted in Nigeria.
171. As an owner, I can pay a flat monthly fee and never be charged per run, so a busy month does not surprise me.
172. As an owner, I can see on one page what I used against what my plan allows, and be told before I hit a limit.
173. As an owner, I can see WhatsApp message costs beyond my allowance passed through at cost, line by line.
174. As an owner, I can leave at any time and take every record and every automation definition with me.

## T. Accountants and advisers

175. As an accountant, I can be emailed every client's monthly payments, receipts and statements, reconciled, without logging in anywhere.
176. As an accountant, I can have VAT and withholding tax computed on each invoice and the remittance prepared for approval.
177. As an accountant, I can have payroll deductions (PAYE, pension, NHF) computed and remitted on their dates after approval.
178. As an accountant, I can serve twenty clients from one account, switching between them in chat.
179. As an auditor, I can verify the audit log's chain for any period independently.

## U. Developers at the business

180. As a developer, I can read and write the business's records through a documented REST API with row-level permissions, from a browser or a phone app, with no server of my own.
181. As a developer, I can sign customers in by phone code (SMS or WhatsApp), email or Google, with MFA, using the SDK.
182. As a developer, I can store files with signed URLs and image transforms.
183. As a developer, I can subscribe to row changes in realtime, filtered by what the user may see.
184. As a developer, I can branch the database to test a change and merge it back.
185. As a developer, I can write a workflow in TypeScript, keep it in Git, run its tests in CI and deploy it through the CLI.
186. As a developer, I can trigger a workflow from my app by a signed webhook and read the run's result by API.
187. As a developer, I can migrate from Supabase with users, passwords, files and policies intact.
188. As a developer, I can see per-table API docs generated from the schema.

## V. The platform operator (the holdco's own benefit)

189. As the operator, I can run Xenibase's dunning, onboarding and incident alerts as Taskiem workflows, and retire the custom code.
190. As the operator, I can see every tenant's plan, usage and health in one console, with every action I take recorded.
191. As the operator, I can review a third-party connector against a checklist, with a second reviewer required, before it is published.
192. As the operator, I can declare an incident from a phone and have every affected customer told.
193. As the operator, I can measure signup-to-first-run and the share of SMEs that reach it, by channel and by language.
194. As the operator, I can grow the AI evaluation suite from real, anonymised requests and gate every prompt or model change on it.
195. As the operator, I can price a chat-only tier, a Starter, a Growth and a Business plan in naira, change prices without a deploy, and invoice with VAT.
196. As the operator, I can offer the two products as one bundle on one invoice, while each still works alone.

## W. Stories across all of the above

197. As anyone, I can do everything on WhatsApp that I can do on the web, with the same permissions and the same audit trail.
198. As anyone, I can get every outcome, alert and approval on WhatsApp, inside the twenty-four-hour window as a reply and outside it as an approved template.
199. As anyone, I can see only the last four digits of any account or card in any message, and never a BVN or NIN.
200. As anyone, I can use the product in Nigerian Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa or Igbo, reviewed by native speakers, by text or by voice.
201. As anyone on a feature phone, I can use USSD for the menus and SMS for the outcomes.
202. As anyone, I can be rate-limited rather than locked out when my phone sends too much, and have every start need an explicit **yes**.
203. As anyone, I can unlink my number in one step if my phone is lost, and everything stops.
204. As anyone, I can ask for help in chat and reach a person when the agent cannot answer.
