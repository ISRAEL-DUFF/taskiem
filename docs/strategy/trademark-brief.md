# Trademark brief for the agent

Date: 2026-10-26 · Status: ready to send to an accredited Nigerian trademark agent · Register item: [L3](../needs-people.md)

## The applicant

**ISPEND DIGITAL SERVICES LTD**, a Nigerian company. Taskiem is a product of the company, not a separate company. The company signs the power of attorney and owns the mark.

## The mark

**TASKIEM**, as a word mark. A logo mark follows once the brand is chosen; the word mark comes first.

Backup names, searched in the same request so a refusal does not cost another week: *to be added by the founder (two)*.

## What Taskiem is

Software that lets a business automate routine work, mostly by chatting on WhatsApp: reminding customers who owe money, sending receipts, paying staff after an owner approves, collecting school fees into an account number per student, reconciling POS terminal sales, and a one-page online shop with orders and bookings. Sold as a monthly subscription in naira, hosted in Nigeria. Launch market Nigeria; Ghana and Kenya possibly later.

## What we ask for

1. An availability search at the Trademarks Registry for **TASKIEM** (and each backup) in these classes:

   | Class | Covers, for us |
   | --- | --- |
   | 9 | Downloadable software and mobile applications |
   | 42 | Software as a service; hosting; designing and developing software |
   | 35 | Business management and administration services; invoicing; online shop services for others |
   | 36 | Financial services: payment collection and disbursement, fee collection, reconciliation |
   | 38 | Telecommunication services: messaging by WhatsApp, SMS and USSD |

2. Your view on how distinctive the name is, given other marks beginning with "Task".
3. If the name is clear, a quote and timeline to file in all five classes, and whether you recommend a separate application per class.
4. Whether ISPEND DIGITAL SERVICES LTD should also register "Taskiem" as a business name with the Corporate Affairs Commission, since it will trade under that name.
5. Optional, priced separately: searches in Ghana and Kenya, and whether to file there now or within the priority period.

## Already checked by us (26 October 2026)

A web search found no product called Taskiem. Similar names exist: Taskiee (an iPhone task app), taskim (an open-source task manager) and Taskia (a Spanish jobs app).
