## Chatting with other people

### Who may use it

You must be 18 or over to use Taskiem, including chats with people.

### Your messages

- You are responsible for what you send. You keep your rights in it; you allow Taskiem to keep, seal, deliver and show it to the people you send it to, on every device they and you sign in on, and to use it as the privacy notice says (safety checks, reports, moderation, the law). **[counsel: licence wording.]**
- Taskiem's servers can read chats between people; they are not end-to-end encrypted. See the privacy notice.
- A message you send to someone new is a request: they choose whether to accept it. There are limits on how many requests you may send, which grow as your account ages and as people accept your requests.

### What is not allowed

In chats with people you must not:

- scam or defraud anyone, ask for codes, PINs, BVNs or card details, or pretend to be a business, a bank or Taskiem;
- threaten, harass, bully or stalk anyone, or send hateful content;
- send sexual content to anyone who has not asked for it, or any sexual content involving children;
- send spam, chain messages or the same message to many people who did not ask for it;
- sell or promote anything illegal, or break someone else's rights;
- use more than one account to get round a limit, a block or a moderator's decision.

**[counsel: align with the moderation policy, PC6.]**

### Reports and moderators' decisions

- You can report a person or a chat. With your agreement, the last 20 messages of the chat, with the photos and files in them, go to Taskiem's moderators.
- Taskiem's automated checks may hold a message to someone new that looks like a scam; it is not delivered unless a moderator releases it.
- If you break these terms, Taskiem's moderators may warn you, limit how many message requests you can send, suspend your account from chatting with people, or close your account. We tell you in the app, with the reason. A closure takes effect 30 days after it is decided unless you appeal or we lift it.
- You can appeal each decision once, in the app (Settings, Your standing). A different moderator reads your appeal, and we tell you what they decide. **[counsel: other remedies; the regulator; disputes clause.]**

### Money between people

Taskiem moves no money between people. "Your share" cards show an account to pay into; paying is between you and the other person, through your own bank. Taskiem is not responsible for a payment you make to another person. **[counsel: wording; consumer-protection limits.]**

### Requests from the authorities

Taskiem gives data to the authorities only under proper legal process, as the privacy notice says.

### Ending

You can delete a chat or your account at any time. Deleting your copy does not delete the other person's.
