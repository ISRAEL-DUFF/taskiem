# Connect Taskiem to your Payrolla account

Taskiem can follow your payroll, send approval and payslip reminders, and, if you allow it, show Payrolla inside Taskiem. Only you, the company owner, can connect it, and you can disconnect it at any time.

**Taskiem can never move money or approve payroll on its own.** Approving and paying always need a person's own confirmation in Payrolla.

## 1. Create Taskiem's key in Payrolla

1. Sign in at https://payroller.surge.sh and go to **Settings → Connected apps**.
2. Select **Connect an app**, name it `Taskiem`, and choose **An app we use**.
3. Tick what Taskiem may do:

   | Option | What it allows |
   |---|---|
   | **Read payroll** | See runs, totals and who has been paid |
   | **Read team** | See names and contact details. Never bank details, ID numbers or pay. |
   | **Receive events** | Get told the moment something happens |
   | **Send payslip links** | Send employees a link to their payslip |
   | **Embed payroll** | Show Payrolla's screens inside Taskiem |

4. Confirm with your password and authenticator code.
5. **Copy the key** that appears. It's shown once.

## 2. Enter it in Taskiem

Open Taskiem's **Connections**, choose **Payrolla**, and paste the key. Don't send the key to anyone by email or chat, including Taskiem's staff or ours.

## 3. Optional settings in Payrolla

These are all under **Settings → Connected apps**:

- **Send payslip links.** Switch on **Let connected apps send payslip links** if Taskiem should message employees their payslips. Employees confirm the last four digits of their phone before a payslip opens.
- **Show payroll inside Taskiem.** Open **Taskiem → Embedding** and set:
  - **Where it may appear:** `https://app.taskiem.com`, plus any other address Taskiem gives you.
  - **What it may show:** the full app, or single screens such as reviewing a run or paying people.
  - **Who can sign in:** only your current Payrolla team, or new people too, up to a role you choose.
  - **Approving and paying:**
    - **Confirm with a code** is the default and the one we recommend. Each approval or payment needs a code sent to that person's own phone, email or authenticator.
    - **Trust the app's sign-in** skips the code. Choose it only if you're sure about how your team signs in to Taskiem.
- **Payment confirmation.** Under **Payment confirmation**, check that everyone who approves or pays can receive a code. For anyone marked **Set up**, enter their phone number, and they read you the code we text them.
  - **Changing a number:** if you change someone's number later, they're told by email and on their old phone.

## Checking on it, or disconnecting

Open **Settings → Connected apps → Taskiem** to:
- see when Taskiem last used its key;
- see the events it receives and whether they arrived;
- **Revoke access**. Taskiem stops working with your account immediately, including any screens open inside Taskiem.
