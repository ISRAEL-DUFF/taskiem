# Your stock

If you sell from a shop, Taskiem keeps how many of each item you have, from WhatsApp or the web. Every change is kept, so you can always see why a count is what it is.

## Starting

On WhatsApp, send what you have, item by item:

*add 50 cartons of Indomie at 8,500*

That is how many, the unit, the item, and what you paid for one. The first time, Taskiem asks if you want to start keeping stock; reply **yes**. It makes a products table for your items. You need no page for it.

On the web, open **Storefront > Stock** and press **Keep my stock**, then **Add items**. If you already have a catalogue for your page, Taskiem keeps the count there, adding the columns it needs.

## Every day

- *add 20 cartons of Indomie* when goods come in.
- *how many bags of rice do I have?* or *stock rice* for one item's count and its last change.
- *stock* for what is low.
- *adjust rice -2 spoilt* when some are spoilt, lost or found (*+3 found*).
- *stock rice 12* when you count what is on the shelf.

Each time, Taskiem shows you the change first. Reply **yes** to make it.

When a customer's order is accepted on your page, the stock goes down by itself.

## Sales

When you sell, send what you sold, the amount and how you were paid:

*sold 3 bags of rice 45,000 cash*

Use *transfer* or *pos* instead of *cash* for those. Several items go in one message: *sold 2 cartons indomie, 1 bag rice 60,000 transfer*. Leave out the amount and Taskiem uses your prices. You can say it in Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa or Igbo too: *i don sell 3 bags rice 45,000 cash*.

Reply **yes**, and the items come off your stock. Cash goes into your money record at once. A transfer or a terminal sale waits until the money comes in, then Taskiem matches them.

On the web, press **Record a sale** on the Stock page. Today's sales are at the top.

Each evening after you close, Taskiem sends you the day's total: how much, by cash, transfer and terminal, what sold most, and what is low. If your terminal took money that nobody said the items for, it says so: press **Say what was sold** on the Stock page, or send *sold* with the items, the amount and *pos*.

## Buying stock

Add the people you buy from, with the items they supply:

*add supplier Dangote Depot 0803 123 4567 for rice, indomie*

They get a link by text message. One tap, and your orders reach them on WhatsApp.

When items run low, send *order from Dangote*. Taskiem puts the low items into one order, with how many to buy and what you paid last time. Reply **yes** and it goes to the supplier, who taps **Confirm**, or **Change** to say what they can bring instead. You hear either way.

When the goods come, send *received 10 bags of rice*. Your stock goes up, and the item's cost price becomes what you paid. When you pay the supplier, send *paid Dangote 425,000 transfer*: it goes into your money record, and *suppliers* shows what you still owe.

On the web, the Stock page has **Orders to suppliers** with **Order what is low**, and **Storefront > Suppliers** keeps your suppliers. If more than one person runs your shop, set **Approving orders** there: a big order is then sent by someone other than the person who drafted it.

## Low stock

Give each item a **reorder level** on the Stock page: when the count is at that or less, the item is low. Give it an **order up to** level too, and Taskiem says how many to order. *stock* lists what is low, and each morning's money summary says it too.

## When the count looks wrong

Open **Storefront > Stock** and press **History** on the item. Every change is there: goods received, orders, adjustments with their reasons, counts. If someone changed the number in your products table by hand, it shows as **Changed in the table**.
