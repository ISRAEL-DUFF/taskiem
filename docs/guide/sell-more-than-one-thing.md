# Sell more than one thing

Your page can have more than your shop. A shop that also cooks can have a menu page; a tailor who hires out chairs, a Hire page; a church with a bookshop and a hall, a page for each. Each extra page is a **front**: it has its own list, its own form, and its own workflow, and it sits on your one page, with your one money record and your one WhatsApp number. Customers see your home page with a card for each front.

## Pick what kind of thing it is

When you add a front, you say what it is for. Taskiem then makes its list and its form for you:

| Kind | For | Customers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Shop** | Products | Order, and ask questions |
| **Food** | A menu: dishes, sizes and extras, how long each takes | Order, for pickup or delivery |
| **Hire** | Chairs, canopies, cooking pots, generators | Pick the days and how many, and pay a deposit |
| **Spaces** | A hall, an event centre, a room | Book a day or some hours, and pay a deposit |
| **Tickets** | A concert, a seminar, a party | Buy tickets; each seat gets a code for the door |
| **Classes** | A course, lessons, a workshop | Take a place, paying in full or in parts |
| **Quotes** | Tailoring, catering, printing, anything made to order | Ask for a price; you send one and they accept |
| **Questions**, **Call-backs** | A question, a call back | Send it |
| **Custom** | Anything else | The forms you add |

## From WhatsApp

1. Send *add hire front Canopies* (or *add front Canopies* and pick the kind from the list). Reply *yes*.
2. Add what you hire out: *add item Canopy 5000 to Canopies*. It starts with a count of one; change how many you have in its table.
3. Turn its form on: *turn on hire for Canopies*. Taskiem sets up its workflow for you.
4. Turn the page on: *front Canopies on*.

The words are the same for the others: *add dish …* for Food, *turn on bookings for Hall*, *turn on tickets for Concert*, *turn on enrolment for Courses*, *turn on quotes for Tailoring*. Send *fronts* to see them all.

## Every day

- **Hire**: you are asked *Are the items back?* when they are due. Approve when everything is back: a late return adds the late fee, and the customer is sent a link for what is left.
- **Tickets**: at the door, send *check in AK-7QX4KD-2* for each code. A code goes in once. *tickets left* shows what is left and how many are in.
- **Classes**: *places left* shows each class. When a class is full, people can join its waiting list; you are asked to offer a place when one frees.
- **Quotes**: you get each request on WhatsApp with what they asked. Reply *quote AK-7QX4KD 45000* to send a price. *quotes* lists the requests still waiting.
- **Orders**: *orders Canopies* shows one front's orders; the Orders page in the web app has a **From** filter.

## On the web

**Storefront > Fronts** shows your fronts. **Add a front** asks the name and the kind; each front has its own page where you **Add an item** (name, price, photo), hide, change or remove items, and its forms (**Set up** makes a form's workflow) and **Turn on**. Customers tap an item to see it and **Add to order**; if orders are not on yet, the front's page shows **Turn on orders**.

## Good to know

- Only your shop's products keep [stock](your-stock.md). Hire items, halls, tickets and places are held and given back as people book and return; nothing on another front changes your stock.
- Money from every front goes into the same record and is matched the same way, so the Money page shows it all.
- Taking payment when people order (tickets and classes are paid when they are taken) needs a verified business, as it does for your shop.
