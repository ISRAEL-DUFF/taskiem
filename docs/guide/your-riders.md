# Your riders

If you deliver your shop's orders with riders of your own, Taskiem sends each order to a rider on WhatsApp, tells your customer where it is, takes payment at the door, and pays your riders every week.

## Setting up

1. **Your delivery areas.** Open **Storefront > Forms > Order**, turn on delivery, and list the areas you deliver to with each one's delivery fee: "Lekki Phase 1, ₦1,500". Customers choose their area and see the fee before they order. Tick **Customers may pay at the door** if you take payment on delivery.
2. **Your riders.** Open **Storefront > Riders** and press **Add a rider**: their name, mobile number, the areas they cover and what you pay them per delivery. They get a text with a link; one tap opens WhatsApp, and from then on your deliveries reach them there.
3. **Deliveries by themselves.** Open **Storefront > Deliveries** and press **Turn on deliveries**. Every order for delivery you accept then goes to your riders by itself. Without it, you send each order from its page. On WhatsApp, send *turn on deliveries*. If you changed your order workflow yourself, Taskiem asks before replacing it.

Riders tell Taskiem when they are working: they send *off*, *on* or *busy till 3pm*. It counts for every business they ride for, and you never see which.

## An order on its way

When you accept an order for delivery, Taskiem offers it to a free rider who covers the area. They have a few minutes to tap **Accept**. If they can't, or don't answer, it goes to the next rider. If nobody takes it, you are told.

You can also send an order yourself: on the order's page, press **Offer to a free rider** or **Choose the rider**. On WhatsApp, send *order AK-7QX4KD to Musa*. **Take it back** (or *release AK-7QX4KD*) takes it from the rider.

The rider taps **Picked up**, **Arrived** and **Delivered**, and your customer is texted each time. **Delivered** completes the order. The order's page shows each step and its time, with the rider's photo if you ask riders for one. **Deliveries** lists the day's, late ones first. If a rider is slow to pick up or to arrive, you are told with their number, so you can call them.

## Paying at the door

When the rider taps **Arrived** on an order not yet paid, Taskiem gives your customer an account number for that order. The rider gets it too, to show them. When the money lands, the rider is told **Paid. Hand it over.** The money is counted to the order by itself, and it never passes through the rider.

If the customer pays cash, the rider taps **Cash received**. The rider then owes you that cash until they bring it:

- A transfer from them naming the order counts by itself.
- Cash they hand you, you record under **Riders > Pay riders > Record cash handed in**.
- Anything still owed is taken off their pay.

If a rider owes more than your limit (₦20,000 unless you change it), it shows under **Money > Leaks**.

## Paying your riders

Open **Riders > Pay riders**. You see what each rider is due (their fees, less any cash they owe), and add each rider's bank account. Adding or changing one asks you to confirm it is you, with your passkey or authenticator app; if you have neither, add one under **Account** first.

Press **Set up weekly pay** and choose a day and time. Each week:

1. Taskiem checks each rider's account name with their bank.
2. It asks you to approve the list, in WhatsApp or **Approvals**.
3. It pays each rider once, from your Taskiem wallet.

**Pay riders now** does the same at once. A rider with no bank account is left out and named, and paid next time.

If a payout stays **Being paid** because its run was stopped, check your bank. If the rider was not paid, press **Mark not paid** on it, and they are paid in the next payout. Only do this once the bank shows the money did not go, or they are paid twice.

## Ratings

After a delivery, your customer is asked to rate it from 1 to 5. Each rider's average shows on the Riders page and in your morning summary. If a rider's ratings fall below 3, you are told.

## Settings

On **Deliveries**, **How orders are offered** sets:

- whether orders go to riders in turn, or you are asked in WhatsApp who takes each one;
- how many minutes a rider has to accept;
- how long picking up and getting to the customer may take;
- whether riders send a photo at the door;
- how much cash a rider may owe;
- the rating below which you are told.
