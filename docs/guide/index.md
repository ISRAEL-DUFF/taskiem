# Taskiem for business owners

Plain how-tos for running your business on Taskiem: no code and no technical steps. The developer documentation is elsewhere in these docs; you do not need it.

- [Your first workflow](start.md): pick a template or describe what you want.
- [Templates](templates.md): filling in a template's details.
- [Connecting your apps](connections.md): Paystack, Google Sheets, WhatsApp and the rest.
- [Paying people](paying-people.md): staff and suppliers by bank and account number.
- [Your riders](your-riders.md): orders sent to your riders on WhatsApp, paying at the door, cash riders owe, weekly pay and ratings.
- [Running a school](schools.md): students, term fees, each child's own account number, receipts, the family page, chasing, clearance and the gate, and the daily close.
- [Your tables](tables.md): keeping records in Taskiem instead of a spreadsheet.
- [Changing a workflow](changing-a-workflow.md): when it runs, values from earlier steps, and "run only when".
- [Settings](settings.md): the settings your workflows read, and the Advanced switch.
- [Your own domain](own-domain.md): your page on a domain you own, step by step for your registrar.
- [On your website](your-website.md): your page on a website you already have, and sending the details to your web designer.
- [Your own WhatsApp number](own-whatsapp-number.md): messages from your own number instead of the shared one.
- [On your phone](on-your-phone.md): Taskiem on your home screen like an app, and approving from Home.
