# Templates

A template is a ready workflow. You fill in its details; Taskiem makes a draft you can look over and publish.

The details are asked the way you know them:

- **A spreadsheet:** paste its link from your browser's address bar, then press **Check it**. Taskiem shows the sheet's name.
- **A tab:** choose it from the list of the sheet's tabs. The first row of the tab names the columns the template says, such as *name, phone, amount_owed*.
- **Amounts** are in naira.
- **Phone numbers** can be written any way: *0803 123 4567*, *+234 803 123 4567* or *2348031234567*.
- **A WhatsApp template:** choose it by what it says. Its language is filled in for you. (This needs your WhatsApp Business Account ID on the WhatsApp connection.)
- **A table and its columns:** choose them from your tables.
- **Who approves:** choose a role, such as the owner.

Templates that move money always ask for an approval first.
