# Your own domain

Already own a domain such as *adakitchen.ng*? Your page can show on it. This is optional: your page already has a Taskiem address that works.

1. Open **Storefront**, then **Your own domain**, type your domain and press **Add domain**.
2. Choose **where you bought it**: Whogohost, Qservers, DomainKing, GoDaddy, Namecheap or another company. Taskiem shows the steps for that company's screens, with exactly what to type in each box:
   - first a **TXT** record that proves the domain is yours (its name is *_taskiem-verify*);
   - then the records that send visitors to your page (*@* means the domain itself, *www* the www. address).
3. Press **Check now**. Taskiem says which record is there and which is not, for example *"The TXT record named _taskiem-verify is not there yet"* or *"adakitchen.ng points somewhere else"*. Records can take from a few minutes to a few hours to show.
4. Once the proof is there, your page shows on your domain.

**Keep the TXT record.** Taskiem checks it every few hours. If it goes missing for over a day, your page stops showing on the domain (it still works at its Taskiem address) and Taskiem tells you on WhatsApp or Telegram what to check.
