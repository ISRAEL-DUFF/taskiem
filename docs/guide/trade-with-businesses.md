# Trade with businesses on Taskiem

If a supplier or a customer of yours is on Taskiem too, you can **link** your two businesses. Then their invoices arrive as bills you can pay from Taskiem, your orders arrive in their business as orders, and you talk in one chat, on the web and on your phone. Nobody retypes anything, and each of you keeps your own records.

## Finding and linking

On the web, open **Chat**. The search box above the list finds businesses on Taskiem: type a name (*Dangote*), or what you need and where (*cement in Ikeja*, *who sells canopies*). Tap a business, say how you trade (**They supply us**, **They buy from us** or **Both**) and press **Request link**. On WhatsApp or Telegram, send *link Dangote* or *find cement in Ikeja*, reply with the number of the business, and tap how you trade.

The other business gets your request with **Accept**, **Decline** and **Block**. Nothing crosses until they accept. Only an owner or admin can ask or accept.

Three more ways to link:

- When you send an invoice or an order to a business that is on Taskiem, the message carries **Link with us on Taskiem**. Tapping it is the request.
- Every business has a link code, on its invoices, receipts and printable card, and under **Linked businesses** in Settings. Opening it while signed in starts the request.
- Send *link 0803…* with a phone number, or a storefront address. The request goes to the business behind it, if there is one. You are told nothing else, so a number cannot be used to find out who is on Taskiem.

To be found by name or by what you sell, turn on **Let other businesses find us** (send *find us*; *hide us* turns it off). It is on already if you have published a storefront, because everything a search shows is on your page.

**Linked businesses** in Settings lists your links and requests. There you can end a link, withdraw a request or unblock a business. Ending a link stops new documents; what already crossed stays on both sides.

## The Chat page

**Chat** works like WhatsApp Web: your conversations on the left, one per linked business, the open one on the right. Type and press Enter; reply to a message by swiping it or pressing **Reply**; attach a photo or a PDF, or hold the microphone for a voice note. Mute, archive or pin a conversation from its menu. Search in a conversation from the box above it.

Everything between the two businesses is in the conversation: your messages, and every order, bill, delivery and payment as a card at the moment it happened. A card has what you can do with it: **Accept** or **Decline** on a new order, **Pay** on a bill, **All came** on a delivery. Reply to a card to talk about that document; the reply shows on the document's page too.

As you type, **@** suggests your linked businesses and **#** the items on a linked supplier's price list, with the price. In the **Taskiem** conversation at the top of the list (your own chat with Taskiem, the same one you have on WhatsApp), *Buy 400 bags of cement from @Dangote and 20 blocks from @Cherry* reads back as two orders, and one **Yes** sends both.

On your phone, the same messages reach you on WhatsApp or Telegram with the business's name in front: *[Dangote Stores] Musa: the rice is ready*. Reply by quoting the message, or send *ask Dangote when is the rice coming*.

To write to a linked business from your Taskiem chat, start the message with *...*. Put it on its own line, or before the message on the same line:

*... @Dangote how much is a bag of cement?*

The message goes straight to Dangote, and Taskiem only answers *✓ Sent to Dangote Nigeria Ltd*. Mention several businesses to send the same words to each. A message without *...* is read as always: *Buy 400 bags of cement from @Dangote* still makes an order.

You don't have to type a business's whole name: *@Dangote* finds *Dangote Nigeria Ltd*. If two of your linked businesses start the same way, Taskiem asks which one, and sends nothing until you say more of the name. Owners, admins and operators can write; everyone else can read.

## Bills and paying

When a linked supplier sends you an invoice, it arrives as a **bill**: under **Bills** on the web, as a card in the conversation, and as a message in your chat. *bills* lists the bills you still have to pay; *bill INV-0042* shows one, with the account number to pay into.

**Pay** on the bill pays it from the account you connected to Taskiem, after the approval your business requires. The first time, press **Set up paying bills** on the Bills page and choose the account. The supplier's invoice is paid the moment the money lands, and your bill with it. You can also pay from your bank app into the account number on the bill, which was made for your business alone: the supplier's Taskiem knows it is you, and your bill is marked paid when they count it. If you paid some other way, press **Paid outside Taskiem** and say how much; the bill shows what you say and what the supplier has counted, side by side.

If a bill is wrong, press **Query this bill** and say why. A queried bill is not paid by itself and not reminded until the supplier settles the query, as it stands or with an amount off.

Your morning summary says **Bills due** when any are due within three days or overdue; *bills due* in chat lists them. When a supplier reminds you of a bill, the reminder comes with **Pay**.

## Orders from a linked supplier

On **Suppliers**, mark the supplier **On Taskiem** and choose the link. From then on, an order to them (drafted by hand, from the low list, or by *order 20 bags of rice from Dangote*) arrives in their business as an order, and what they do with it comes back to your order: accepted, changed, ready, on its way, delivered. Say under **Where linked suppliers deliver** where their rider should bring your orders.

If they change a quantity or a price, you get **Accept the change** or you can cancel. Before they accept, you can cancel freely; after, cancelling is a request they accept or decline. When the delivery comes, press **All came**, or **Not all came** and say what came. If you press neither, their count is taken a day later and your stock goes up by it.

Their invoice for the order becomes your bill, raised when they accept the order or when it is delivered, as they choose.

## Price lists

A linked supplier can share a **price list** with you: their items at your prices, with a minimum quantity and whether each is available. See it with **Prices** on their row, or *prices from Dangote*, and order from it there. The first time you order an item, say which of your own items it is; after that, your orders reach them as their own items, and the cost price of your item follows theirs.

To share your prices with the businesses you supply, open **Storefront > Price lists**, add items from your stock with their prices, and choose which linked businesses see the list. One business can have its own prices on top.

## Credit terms

For a business you supply, set **Days to pay** and a **Credit limit** with its orders. Every invoice to them is due that many days after it is raised. If an order would take what they owe you past the limit, it waits on your Trade orders page, flagged, instead of being accepted by itself; you can still accept it. They are told why it waits and shown their bills.

For a supplier, you see the terms they gave you, what you owe, and what is left to use, under **Statement** on their row. Both sides see the same figures.

## Workflows for linked businesses

Four templates work between linked businesses. Each needs a linked supplier or customer, so it is offered once you have one:

- **Pay bills from linked suppliers after one approval**: every week, on the day you choose, the bills due that week up to an amount, each paid after one approval; the rest held and named.
- **Reorder from your suppliers on Taskiem**: every morning, what is low, as one order per linked supplier at their prices, shown to you as a list and sent when you approve.
- **Tell me when a linked customer orders**: a message each time one does.
- **Accept and dispatch linked customers' orders**: an order up to an amount and within the customer's credit limit is accepted, sent to a rider and invoiced without a tap.

You can also describe what you want: *pay Dangote's bills every Friday after I approve*, *reorder from my linked suppliers*.

## What the other side can and cannot see

A linked business sees your business's name, and the documents between you: the invoices you send it, the orders it places with you or you with it, the messages, photos and voice notes in your conversation, and the price list you share with it. It sees what you have paid it, and what you say you paid outside Taskiem.

It cannot see your other customers or suppliers, your stock, your money log, your members or their phone numbers, what you pay anyone else, or any price list you did not share with it. Messages show the business's name with the sender's first name only. A photo or voice note is copied into each business's own storage, so neither side reads the other's files.

Neither business can reach into the other: every document crossing the link is a copy written by Taskiem, recorded in both businesses' audit logs, and the figures on both sides are checked against each other every hour. Ending or blocking a link stops anything new from crossing.
