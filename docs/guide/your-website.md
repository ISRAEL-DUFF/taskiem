# On your website

If you already have a website, your page (catalogue, orders and bookings) can show on it.

1. Open **Storefront**, then **On your website**, type the website's address and choose how to prove it is yours:
   - *I can add a record where I bought the domain*: a TXT record, with the same step-by-step guide as for [your own domain](own-domain.md);
   - *Whoever built the website can upload a file to it*.
2. **Send to my web designer**: type their email and press **Send**. They get the code that shows your page and how to prove the website is yours. You do not need to understand either.
3. When they say it is done, press **Check now**.

If the proof goes missing for over a day, your page stops showing on the website and Taskiem tells you on WhatsApp or Telegram.
