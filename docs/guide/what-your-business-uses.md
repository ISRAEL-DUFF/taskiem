# What your business uses

Taskiem is for every kind of business, so you turn on only the parts you use. Workflows, forms, tables, approvals, chat, your people, billing and settings are always there. These parts are yours to switch:

| Part | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Online shop | Your shop page and its look, other pages, bookings, riders and deliveries |
| Orders | Orders from your shop, refunds and quotes, and orders to and from businesses you are linked with |
| Stock | What you have in stock, your suppliers and price lists |
| Money and invoices | Invoices, money received, bills you owe and your money reports |
| Linked businesses | Finding and linking with other businesses on Taskiem, and chatting with them |
| Schools | Students, families, classes, terms and school fees |

## When you sign up

Taskiem asks what your business does: it sells things, bills customers for a service, runs a school, or just needs workflows for now. That sets which parts you start with. A business that sells things starts with the shop, orders, stock, money and linked businesses; one that bills for a service starts with money and linked businesses; a school starts with Schools and money; "just workflows" starts with none of them.

## Changing it

An owner opens **More, What your business uses**, and turns a part on or off. Anyone in the business can see the list.

- **Turning a part on** turns on what it needs: the shop needs Orders, and Schools need Money.
- **Turning a part off** hides it. **Nothing is deleted**: turn it on again and it is all back.
- A part another one needs is locked while that one is on. Turn the one that needs it off first.
- If workflows of yours use the part, Taskiem lists them first. If you turn it off anyway, those workflows stop at that step, with a plain message, and work again when you turn it back on.

## What turning a part off does

- Its pages leave the menu, and the web address shows that it is not turned on.
- Its words in WhatsApp, Telegram and the web chat are not understood any more.
- Turning the shop off closes your public page ("This shop is not taking orders at the moment") and opens it again when you turn it back on, unless you had closed it yourself.
- The summaries Taskiem sends you by itself (money summaries, the evening stock total) stop for a part that is off.
- Invoices you already sent still have their pages, so customers can still pay them, and money that is on its way is still matched.
- A business you are linked with cannot send you the documents of a part you turned off: an invoice, an order, a price list, a message. An invoice waits and arrives when you turn the part back on.
- With Linked businesses off, other businesses cannot find you in their search. Your existing links stay.
