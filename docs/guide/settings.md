# Settings

**Settings your workflows read** are things such as your phone number or your business name. Type them as you would anywhere: a phone number in any form, a number, or yes or no.

**Advanced** (owners and admins): turns on the technical parts of the web app for someone who builds workflows by hand: a blank canvas, formulas, the test environment and your own connectors. Everyone in the business sees the same. Plans above Shop have it on to begin with.
