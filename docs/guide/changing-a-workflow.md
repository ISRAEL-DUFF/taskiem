# Changing a workflow

Open a workflow: its steps are cards, left to right, after what starts it. Press a card to open its window: what came in on the left, its settings in the middle, what came out on the right. Each step can be changed without writing anything technical.

- **Add a step:** press a **+** on an arrow, or after the last card, and pick from **What happens next?**. Search for it ("send a WhatsApp", "payment link") or open a group.
- **Yes or No:** add an **If** and choose when it goes the Yes way. Add steps to Yes, to No, or to both.
- **A message with a customer's details in it:** type the words, then press **Add a field** (or type **/**) to put in a value, such as the customer's name. You can also drag a value from **What came in**.
- **Try it first:** press **Test workflow**. Each step runs with sample data and gets a green tick; nothing is sent and no money moves. Open a step to see what it gave.
- **Undo** and **Redo** are at the top; **Save draft** keeps your changes, **Publish** makes them live.

- **When it runs** (under **Settings**, *When it runs*): choose *On a schedule* and pick, for example, *Every week, on Friday, at 10:00*. Times are Lagos time.
- **A value from an earlier step:** in a step's details, choose **Use a value from an earlier step** and pick it by its words, such as *From “Read the staff sheet”: The rows* or *What was filled in: Supplier*.
- **Run only when:** add a rule, such as *From “Get the payment approved”: the decision* **is** *approved*, or *What was filled in: amount* **more than** *50000*. Add more rules and choose *All of these* or *Any one of these*.
- **Approvals:** choose who decides, how long they have, what happens if nobody decides, and which details they see.
- **A form step:** choose whose phone or email it goes to, how long to wait and when to remind them.

Parts set up by hand by a developer show *This part was set up by hand*; an owner or admin can turn on **Advanced** in Settings to see them.

**New form** on the Forms page makes a form and the workflow behind it: choose its questions, publish it, and share its link.
