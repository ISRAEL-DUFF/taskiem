# Your tables

A table keeps records in Taskiem, such as your debtors with their phone numbers and balances, instead of a spreadsheet.

- **Make one** under **Tables**, or let a template or *Describe it* make the one it needs.
- **Bring in a spreadsheet:** choose **Make a table from this file** and pick a CSV file. Taskiem matches its columns, and asks about any it cannot place.
- **Add and change rows** in the web app, or from WhatsApp (*add debtors*, *find debtors chidi*).

Phone numbers and emails in a table are personal data: they are hidden in logs and never shown to the AI.
