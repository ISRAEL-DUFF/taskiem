# Your tables

A table keeps records in Taskiem, such as your customers with their phone numbers and areas, instead of a spreadsheet. What customers owe you is kept with your invoices instead: see [Keep your records in chat](keep-your-records-in-chat.md#who-owes-you).

- **Make one** under **Tables**, or let a template or *Describe it* make the one it needs.
- **Bring in a spreadsheet:** choose **Make a table from this file** and pick a CSV file. Taskiem matches its columns, and asks about any it cannot place. Or send the file in the chat.
- **Add and change rows** in the web app, or from WhatsApp and Telegram (*add to customers*, *find customers chidi*, then *change 1*).
- **Ask** in the chat: *how many customers*, *who lives in Ikeja?*

Everything you can do with tables in the chat is in [Keep your records in chat](keep-your-records-in-chat.md).

## Keep a table for some people

Everyone you give access to your tables can see every table's rows. To keep one for some people only (your HR requests, salaries), open it under **Tables** and choose **Keep it for some roles** under **Who can see the rows**: name the roles, such as *hr, hr_lead*, and give those roles to the right people under **Members**. Then only they, and you as the owner, see and change its rows, on the web and in the chat; everyone else still sees the table's name, and who to ask. You need to be allowed to manage roles to change this, and to be able to see the rows yourself.

Your workflows still read and write the table, so whoever can change and publish workflows can reach it too: keep that to people you trust with it.

Phone numbers and emails in a table are personal data: they are hidden in logs and never shown to the AI.
