# Taskiem on your phone

Taskiem works in your phone's browser, and you can put it on your home screen so it opens like an app: full screen, with its own icon, and no address bar.

## Put it on your home screen

**On an Android phone (Chrome):** open Taskiem and sign in. Open the menu (bottom right) and press **Install the app**. If you do not see it, open Chrome's own menu (the three dots) and press **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

**On an iPhone (Safari):** open Taskiem and sign in. Press the Share button (the square with an arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

Taskiem's icon, a lime "t" on dark green, is now on your home screen. You stay signed in the same way as in the browser.

## Notifications when Taskiem is closed

When a business you are linked with writes to you, your phone can show a notification even if Taskiem is closed. Turn it on under **Settings, Notifications, Chat when Taskiem is closed**, with **Notify me on this device when a business writes**. Your browser asks once; say yes.

- **On an iPhone**, add Taskiem to your Home Screen first (Share, then Add to Home Screen) and open it from there: the switch appears once you have.
- **Show what the message says** is its own switch for each device. Off, the notification says only that a business wrote, which suits a phone others can see.
- **Your other devices** are listed under the switch, each with Remove. Signing out takes the device you are on off the list.
- Only people who write in chats are notified, and not for a chat you muted.

## Getting around

- **The bar at the bottom** takes you to Home, Money, Orders and Workflows (the ones your role can see). **Menu** opens everything else.
- **Pull down from the top of a page** to load it again with the latest figures, in the app on your home screen. In a browser tab, the browser's own pull to refresh does the same.
- **Tabs that do not fit**, such as Money's Overview, Leaks, Upload statement, Cash and expenses and Fees, slide sideways. The one you are on is always in view.
- **The (i) button** beside a page's title says what the page is for. It is folded away on a phone so the page opens on what it shows.

## Home and Money

**Home** opens on a green card with the money that came in today, how many payments, a line showing how the day built up, the money that went out, and how many payments still need matching to an invoice. Under it: today's orders and this week's jobs, then **Needs you**.

**Money** shows Today, This week or This month (other periods under **Other**): money in, money out and what you kept, a bar for each day (press one to see its figures), where the money came from, and what to look at.

**Orders, Workflows, Activity and Invoices** are lists you tap: each row shows who or what it is, the amount or time, and its status, and opens the order, workflow, run or invoice. Activity puts today's runs first, then yesterday's; the workflow, environment and date filters are under **More filters**.

**An invoice or an order** opens on a green card with what is still due (or its total), how much is paid, the customer and the date. **A run** opens on a card with its status and how its steps stand, such as "1 done · 1 waiting". The link at the top of each goes back to its list.

**A workflow** opens as a list of its steps, after what starts it: tap one to change it full screen, or **Add a step here** to add one after it. **Test workflow** at the end tries it with sample data.

**Get started** shows how much of your setup is done and the next step, with each step's button beside it.

## Approving from Home

When a payment or another step waits for your approval, it shows under **Needs you** on Home with an **Approve** button. Pressing it opens a sheet showing exactly what you are approving: the amount, who it goes to and their account, when the workflow says them. Check it, then press **Confirm** (or confirm with your passkey if your business asks for one). To reject, or to see more about the request, press the row itself to open **Approvals**.

If the payment is to a bank account your business has never paid through Taskiem, the row says **First payment to this account** and the sheet asks you to check the account number and name with the person you are paying first. A changed account number is a common way money goes to the wrong person.

## The colours

The bar at the top of your phone is dark green in the light theme and follows the page in the dark theme. Choose light, dark or your phone's setting under **Account**.
