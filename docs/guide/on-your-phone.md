# Taskiem on your phone

Taskiem works in your phone's browser, and you can put it on your home screen so it opens like an app: full screen, with its own icon, and no address bar.

## Put it on your home screen

**On an Android phone (Chrome):** open Taskiem and sign in. Open the menu (bottom right) and press **Install the app**. If you do not see it, open Chrome's own menu (the three dots) and press **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

**On an iPhone (Safari):** open Taskiem and sign in. Press the Share button (the square with an arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

Taskiem's icon, a lime "t" on dark green, is now on your home screen. You stay signed in the same way as in the browser.

## Getting around

- **The bar at the bottom** takes you to Home, Money, Orders and Workflows (the ones your role can see). **Menu** opens everything else.
- **Pull down from the top of a page** to load it again with the latest figures, in the app on your home screen. In a browser tab, the browser's own pull to refresh does the same.
- **Tabs that do not fit**, such as Money's Overview, Leaks, Upload statement, Cash and expenses and Fees, slide sideways. The one you are on is always in view.
- **The (i) button** beside a page's title says what the page is for. It is folded away on a phone so the page opens on what it shows.

## Approving from Home

When a payment or another step waits for your approval, it shows under **Needs you** on Home with an **Approve** button. Pressing it opens a sheet showing exactly what you are approving. Check it, then press **Confirm** (or confirm with your passkey if your business asks for one). To reject, or to see more about the request, press the row itself to open **Approvals**.

## The colours

The bar at the top of your phone is dark green in the light theme and follows the page in the dark theme. Choose light, dark or your phone's setting under **Account**.
