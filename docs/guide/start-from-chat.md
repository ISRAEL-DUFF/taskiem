# Start from WhatsApp

You can open your business on Taskiem from WhatsApp, without a laptop, an email or a password. The same works on Taskiem's Telegram bot, and in Slack if your team works there. You can still sign up on the website instead; both are the same Taskiem.

## Open your business

1. Save Taskiem's WhatsApp number and send it **hi**, or a few words in your own language ("I wan open account").
2. Press **Start my business**. Taskiem shows its terms and privacy notice: reply **Yes** to agree. Nothing is made before you do.
3. Answer four questions, one at a time: your business's name, your name, what you sell (a number from the list) and how your customers pay you (one or more numbers). Send **cancel** at any point to stop.
4. Your business is ready. Taskiem offers three automations that fit what you said; the first, **Remind me every day**, needs nothing connected. Tap one, or say what you want in your own words.

On Telegram there is one more step: press **Share my number** so Taskiem knows the number is yours. In Slack, press **Start in Slack** on Taskiem's website and add Taskiem's app to your workspace; the bot then writes to you there with the same questions.

## Connect a service

When an automation needs Paystack, Google Sheets or another service, Taskiem sends a form or a link in the chat. Fill the form, or open the link and type the key there (or sign in to the service). Never type a key into the chat itself: Taskiem ignores it and asks you to change it. When the service is connected, the chat carries on where it left off.

## Keep your chat yours

- Send **pin** to set a six-digit chat PIN. If your number goes quiet for a long time, Taskiem asks for it before doing anything, so a number given to someone else cannot reach your business.
- Send **email you@example.com** to add your email. Open the link Taskiem sends to it: your receipts and invoices then come by email, and you can sign in on the website with it.
- Send **sign in** for a link to choose a password for the website.

## Keep your records

Your customers, your staff, who owes you and any other list live in the chat too: *new table*, *Ada owes 5,000*, *who owes me?*, *how many customers are in Ikeja?* See [Keep your records in chat](keep-your-records-in-chat.md).

## Already use Taskiem on the website?

Write to the number and press **I already use Taskiem**. Send the email you sign in with, and open the link Taskiem emails you while you are signed in. Your chat is then linked to your account and your businesses.

## Bring your people in

Send **invite +2348012345678 as viewer** (or as operator, approver and the other roles you can give on the website) and reply **Yes**. On WhatsApp they get a message with **Join**; on Telegram you get a link to forward to them; in Slack, mention them: **invite @ada as viewer**. They agree to the terms and join. In Slack, people in your workspace can also ask to join; you get **Viewer**, **Operator** or **Decline**.

## Your plan

Send **plan** to see your plan, how many days of the free trial are left and the price. Press **Pay** for a link to pay by card or bank transfer.
