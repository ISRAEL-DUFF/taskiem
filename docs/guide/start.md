# Your first workflow

A workflow is something Taskiem does for you again and again: remind your debtors every Friday, tell you when a payment comes in, pay salaries after you approve.

There are three ways to start, all under **Workflows**.

1. **Start from a template.** Pick one that sounds like what you want, such as *Remind debtors by SMS every week*. Fill in its details (see [Templates](templates.md)) and it becomes a draft.
2. **Describe it.** Write what you want in your own words ("every Friday at 10am, text the customers in my debtors sheet who still owe me"). Taskiem builds the draft and practises it before you save it.
3. **Build it yourself.** Press **New workflow**, give it a name and choose what starts it: *On a schedule*, *A payment comes in*, *An order comes in*, *Someone fills in a form* and more. Then press **+** to add each step from **What happens next?** (see [Changing a workflow](changing-a-workflow.md)).

Then:

- **Connect what it needs.** The draft lists anything still missing, such as your Paystack account, with a button that takes you to the one screen that connects it ([Connecting your apps](connections.md)).
- **Publish it.** A draft does nothing until it is published. Publishing checks it once more.
- **Run it once** to see each step done, or wait for its schedule.

You can do all of this from WhatsApp too: send *build* and what you want.
