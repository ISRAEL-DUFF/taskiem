# Keep your records in chat

You don't need a spreadsheet. Your customers, your staff, what people owe you and any other list can be kept in Taskiem, from WhatsApp or Telegram, and your workflows use them. Nothing is changed until you reply **yes**.

## Your lists

- *new table* starts a list. Taskiem offers the ones it knows (Customers, Staff), or **Something else**: say what the list is called and what you write down for each one ("name, phone, area, amount owed"). It shows you the list and makes it when you say **yes**.
- *add to customers* adds one entry. Taskiem asks for each detail, or opens a short form.
- *find customers ada* shows up to five that match, numbered. Then *change 2* to correct the second one, or *delete 2* to remove it. If someone changed it on the web in the meantime, Taskiem shows you the new values instead of writing over them.
- *add column birthday to customers* and *rename customers to Shop customers* change a list.
- *tables* shows your lists and how many entries each has.

Removing a whole list, or one of its columns, is done on the web, under **Tables**.

## Bring a list you already have

Send the file in the chat: a CSV or Excel file (the first sheet), up to 5,000 rows. Taskiem tells you what is in it and offers **New table** or **Add to table**. If you keep it in Google Sheets, send *copy my sheet* and the sheet's link. It is copied once; after that, keep it in Taskiem.

## Who owes you

Debts are kept with your invoices, so a debt you write down and an invoice you sent are one balance.

- *Ada owes 5,000 for rice* writes it down. The first time, Taskiem asks how long your customers have to pay (7, 14 or 30 days, or no fixed time). If Ada is new, it asks for her phone number so reminders and receipts can reach her; you can skip it.
- *Ada paid 2,000 cash* counts it towards her oldest debt first. Say *transfer* instead of *cash*, and Taskiem asks which account it went into; your bank statement will not count it twice.
- *who owes me?* lists everyone who owes you, the biggest first.
- *balance Ada* shows what Ada owes, line by line.
- *send Ada her balance* sends her one message with everything she owes and a way to pay it all or part: her own account number, or a link.
- *sold 3 bags of rice 45,000 credit to Ada* takes the rice off your stock and writes down what she owes.
- *credit terms 14 days* changes how long customers have to pay.

To remind everyone who owes you, pick the template **Chase overdue invoices every day** with **One message per customer**, or tell Taskiem *remind my customers who owe me*.

## Ask about your lists

- *how many customers* counts a list.
- *total balance* adds up a column of amounts.
- Ask anything else in your own words: *how many customers are in Ikeja?*, *who owes more than 10k?*, *which staff are not active?*

Taskiem answers with the number or the entries, and says how it read your question (*Customers where Area is Ikeja: 14*), so you can see if it understood you. To answer a question like that, Taskiem's AI sees your question and the names of your lists and their columns, never what is in them.

## In your language

All of this works in Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa and Igbo too: *Ada dey owe me 5,000*, *Adé jẹ mí 5,000*, *how many customers dey for Ikeja?* The words in those languages are still being checked by native speakers.

Phone numbers and emails in your lists are personal: Taskiem hides most of each one in the chat (•••567) and never shows them to the AI.
