# Your own WhatsApp number

Taskiem sends your messages from its shared WhatsApp number, with your business's name. Most businesses never need more. Use your own number when customers should see your name as the sender.

You need a phone number that is not already on the WhatsApp app, and about half an hour. Under **Settings**, **WhatsApp number**, open **How to use your own WhatsApp number** for the steps in Meta's screens: making a Meta app, adding the number, and copying four details (the phone number ID, the WhatsApp Business Account ID, an access token and the app secret) into Taskiem.

Taskiem plans to let you do this with one button (Meta's Embedded Signup) once it is approved as a Meta Tech Provider.
