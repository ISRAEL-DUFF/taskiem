# Paying people

Taskiem pays people from your payments account (your Taskiem wallet, or Paystack, Flutterwave, Moniepoint or Anchor), always after the person paying confirms with their passkey or PIN. Nothing is paid from words alone.

**Pay one person.** Open **Pay people** in Tools (or **Pay** in the chat) and choose someone you saved, a rider, a staff member, or type an account number and its bank. Taskiem checks the name with the bank and shows it large before you confirm.

- **Your balance** is at the top of the sheet. With a Taskiem wallet, **Add money** shows its own account number: send money to it from your bank.
- **The fee** shows before you pay: *₦45,000 + ₦25 fee*, and your balance after it. You pay the fee; the person gets the full amount. Where your payments account cannot say the fee first, the sheet says *The fee shows after paying*. If your balance cannot cover the amount and the fee, Taskiem says by how much.
- **Taskiem wallet to Taskiem wallet.** When the account is another business's Taskiem wallet, the money goes wallet to wallet: *Taskiem wallet · arrives at once*, with no fee. If that does not work, it goes by bank as usual.
- **What it is for** (a bill, wages, a delivery, a refund, stock bought, or your own word) goes into your money log, so your reports split by it. The fee is its own line there, as *bank charges*.
- **Tell them in the chat** (riders and staff, on by default): once the bank has it, they see the amount, what it was for, the day and the last four digits of their account. Never your balance, the fee or who approved it. Not for wages or refunds.
- **Send them a receipt**, for someone not on Taskiem: once paid, you get a receipt (a PDF and its link) to send them.
- **Save as a beneficiary**, on by default for an account you typed: give them a name (*chidi*) and next time pay *@chidi* in one tap.

**After you pay**, a line in the chat you paid from says *Paid Chidi Okeke ₦45,000 (delivery) · GTBank ••6789*, then *Received by the bank*. If it fails, the money comes back to your balance and the line says so, with **Try again**.

**Pay with your fingerprint** in the phone app. Turn it on in **Settings › Fingerprint**, or tap **Pay by fingerprint from now on** on the Pay sheet, and confirm it is you with your PIN, your passkey or a code to your phone. From then on, that phone pays with its own fingerprint or face check: up to ₦500,000 a payment and ₦1,000,000 a day. A list of payments, an account changed since you last paid it, and anything that waits for the owner's OK still take your passkey. The fingerprint works only on the phone you turned it on for; on the web you pay with your passkey or PIN, and under **Account › Fingerprint on your phones** you can see and remove those phones.

**From a message.** A message with an account number gets **Pay this account** under it. On WhatsApp, Telegram or Slack, forward the message to Taskiem, or reply *pay* to it: Taskiem checks the name and asks *how much, and for what?*. You confirm by the link, or with your PIN in WhatsApp's form.

**Pay several people** in one list: the foot shows the total, the fees, and your balance after. If the balance is short, it says by how much and which people fit. The list has one line in the chat, and each person their own result.

**A refund to a customer** who paid by transfer: on the order, **Pay it back** opens the sheet with the amount and *a refund* filled in.

A staff member pays only your saved accounts, up to the limit you set; over it, you are asked for your OK. An account that changed since you last paid it always needs your OK. A retry never pays anyone twice.
