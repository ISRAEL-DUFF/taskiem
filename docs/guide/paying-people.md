# Paying people

Taskiem pays staff and suppliers through your Paystack account, always after someone approves.

**One payment** (the *Pay a supplier after approval* template): when you start it, choose the supplier's bank and type their 10-digit account number. Taskiem shows the name on the account, for example *BOLA ADE*. If that is who you mean to pay, press **Yes, pay them**. Nothing is paid until the approver approves.

**Salaries from a sheet** (the *Pay salaries from a sheet* template): the staff tab has these columns in its first row: *name, account_number, bank, amount, phone*. Write the bank as people do (*GTBank*, *First Bank*, *Opay*); write the amount in naira. On payday you approve the whole payroll in one tap, then each person is paid and gets a text.

A retry never pays anyone twice.
