# Pronunciation guide for the narrator

Capitals mark the stressed syllable. These are best-effort spellings of how
the names are normally said in Nigeria; **have a Nigerian team member listen
to the generated voice and correct this file**, then keep it as the single
reference for every voice tool.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Taskiem | TASK-ee-em | Product name; pick one way and never change it |
| Ada | AH-dah | Short, open vowels (not "AY-da") |
