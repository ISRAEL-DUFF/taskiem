# Schools series: the cast

Five new people, all at or around **Bright Hill Academy**, Ikorodu, Lagos. None is in
`video/characters/bible.md`. All are fictional; the bible's style, palette, skin rules,
global prompts and drift checklist (sections 2, 3 and 12) apply to everyone here. Each
master prompt is copied as it is into every generation, with the scene line after it and
the global negative prompt (for Flow: the "guard line" of `video/characters/flow-guide.md`
instead). **Draft for approval:** none has an approved reference sheet yet; make the
sheets first (front, three-quarter, side, expressions) and keep them in
`video/characters/approved/<character>/`.

---

## Mrs Tolulope Fashola: the proprietor

**Who she is.** Proprietor and principal of Bright Hill Academy, Ikorodu: nursery, primary
and junior secondary, about 400 students. A former maths teacher who built the school
from three classrooms. Calm, exact, a little formal; wants to know where the money is
and approves what the rules say she must. Trusts Mr Ibe with the fees office and
checks the figures herself first thing in the morning.

**Age and build.** 54. 1.66 m (5'5"). Upright and composed; unhurried movements.

**Skin.** Warm medium-deep brown with a golden-bronze undertone, mid-tone `#7c4f33`;
luminous, warm gold highlights on the cheekbones, never grey.

**Face.** Oval, high forehead, a firm gentle jaw; fine smile lines at the eyes.

**Eyes.** Dark, steady, almond; they narrow slightly when she reads a figure.

**Brows.** Neat, straight, dark with a hint of grey at the tails.

**Nose and mouth.** Straight, slim nose. Lips medium, a soft rose-brown lipstick; a
slow, warm, closed-mouth smile.

**Hair.** Short natural hair, black with **silver at the temples**, cut close and tidy.
Constant.

**Marks and details.** Thin gold-rimmed reading glasses pushed up into her hair or
held in her hand, **a small gold school-crest brooch** on her lapel, a slim gold watch.

**Signature outfit.** A **deep burgundy blazer** over a cream blouse, a long charcoal
skirt, low black court shoes; a **lime-green silk scarf** knotted at the neck (her
one accent).

**Props.** A phone in a dark case; a leather-bound term planner; a fountain pen.

**Gestures.** Slides her glasses down to read; taps the planner twice with the pen
before she decides; folds her hands when she approves.

**Expressions.** *Default:* calm and attentive. *Reading:* brows together, glasses down.
*Approving:* a slow nod and a small smile. *Concerned:* a held breath, pen paused.

**Voice.** Low, measured alto; clear English with a gentle Yoruba lilt.

**Master prompt (copy exactly):**

> **Mrs Tolulope Fashola**, a 54-year-old **Nigerian woman**, **warm medium-deep brown
> skin with a golden-bronze undertone**, **oval face, high forehead, fine smile lines at
> the eyes**, steady dark almond eyes, neat straight brows with a hint of grey,
> rose-brown lipstick, a slow warm closed-mouth smile; **short natural black hair with
> silver at the temples**; thin gold-rimmed reading glasses, a small gold school-crest
> brooch on her lapel, a slim gold watch; wearing a **deep burgundy blazer over a cream
> blouse, a long charcoal skirt, low black court shoes, a lime-green silk scarf knotted
> at the neck**; upright and composed, calm and exact, a school proprietor in Lagos

## Mr Chukwudi Ibe: the bursar

**Who he is.** Bursar of Bright Hill Academy: runs the fees office, the receipts, the
reminders and the gate list. Patient with parents, precise with money; has kept the
fee books by hand for eleven years and is quietly glad to stop. The main teacher of
the series.

**Age and build.** 47. 1.74 m (5'9"). Solid, slightly stooped from desk work; a calm,
deliberate walk.

**Skin.** Deep brown with a cool-neutral undertone, mid-tone `#53341f`; warm
highlights on the forehead and nose, never ashy.

**Face.** Broad, a high rounded forehead, soft jowls, a strong chin.

**Eyes.** Dark, kind, slightly tired, with fine lines at the corners; they widen when a
figure comes out right.

**Brows.** Thick, level, black.

**Nose and mouth.** Broad nose. Fuller lips; a **neat, short black moustache**; an
easy, patient smile.

**Hair.** Very short black hair with a receding hairline, trimmed close. Constant.

**Marks and details.** **Round tortoiseshell glasses** (round, unlike Ibinabo's square
ones), a plain silver wedding band.

**Signature outfit.** A **pale-blue long-sleeved shirt with the sleeves folded to the
elbow**, a **pine-green tie** with a **lime-green pen** clipped in the shirt pocket
(his one accent), grey trousers, brown leather shoes.

**Props.** A thick green fee ledger; a phone with a grey case; a rubber stamp; a calculator.

**Gestures.** Pushes his glasses up with one knuckle; taps a figure on the ledger with the
pen; turns the phone to show a parent.

**Expressions.** *Default:* patient and attentive. *Counting:* lips move silently.
*Relieved:* a long exhale and a smile. *Firm but kind:* a slow shake of the head, a gentle eye.

**Voice.** Warm baritone, unhurried; clear Igbo-inflected Nigerian English.

**Master prompt (copy exactly):**

> **Mr Chukwudi Ibe**, a 47-year-old **Nigerian man**, **deep brown skin with a
> cool-neutral undertone**, **broad face, high rounded forehead, soft jowls, a strong
> chin**, kind dark slightly tired eyes with fine lines at the corners, thick level
> black brows, **a neat short black moustache**; **very short black hair with a
> receding hairline**; **round tortoiseshell glasses**, a plain silver wedding band;
> wearing a **pale-blue long-sleeved shirt with the sleeves folded to the elbow, a
> pine-green tie, a lime-green pen clipped in the shirt pocket, grey trousers, brown
> leather shoes**; solid and slightly stooped, patient and precise, a school bursar in
> Lagos

## Mallam Audu: the gateman

**Who he is.** Gateman of Bright Hill Academy for nineteen years; knows every child by
name and every parent by their car. On resumption morning he holds one phone at the
gate. Dignified, warm, firm at the gate and gentle with the children.

**Age and build.** 57. 1.78 m (5'10"). Tall, lean, a straight back; unhurried.

**Skin.** Deep brown with a warm reddish undertone, mid-tone `#5d3b28`; weathered,
luminous, warm highlights, never grey.

**Face.** Long, angular, deep laugh lines, a prominent brow.

**Eyes.** Dark, alert, deep-set; they crinkle when he greets a child.

**Brows.** Bushy, black and grey.

**Nose and mouth.** Straight, strong nose. A **short grey-white beard**, neat. A wide,
warm smile.

**Hair.** Mostly covered by a cap; the little showing at the ears is grey-white.

**Marks and details.** A **cream embroidered hula cap** (Hausa style, pale thread). A
silver watch with a worn strap. A **lime-green lanyard** carrying a whistle (his one accent).

**Signature outfit.** A **khaki uniform shirt with pine-green shoulder tabs and a
school-crest patch**, khaki trousers, black boots.

**Props.** A phone in a thick black rugged case, held in two hands; a folding stool at
the gate; a flask of tea.

**Gestures.** Holds the phone at chest height with both hands; lifts one hand to wave a
child in; lowers it, palm out, to say "wait a moment".

**Expressions.** *Default:* alert and kind. *Welcoming:* a wide smile, hand raised.
*Firm:* a level look, palm out. *Reassuring:* a slow nod.

**Voice.** Deep, slow and gentle; Hausa-inflected Nigerian English.

**Master prompt (copy exactly):**

> **Mallam Audu**, a 57-year-old **Nigerian man**, **deep brown skin with a warm
> reddish undertone**, **long angular face, deep laugh lines, a prominent brow**,
> alert deep-set dark eyes, bushy black-and-grey brows, **a short neat grey-white
> beard**, a wide warm smile; **a cream embroidered hula cap** over his hair; a silver
> watch with a worn strap, a lime-green lanyard with a whistle; wearing a **khaki
> uniform shirt with pine-green shoulder tabs and a school-crest patch, khaki
> trousers, black boots**; tall, lean and straight-backed, dignified and gentle, a
> school gateman in Lagos

## Mrs Bimpe Lawal: a parent

**Who she is.** A parent at Bright Hill Academy: mother of Tobi (JSS 1A). A nurse at a
clinic in Ikorodu, on her feet all day; pays her son's fees by transfer, quickly, in
the minutes between shifts. When money is tight she asks, politely, for a plan.

**Age and build.** 38. 1.62 m (5'4"). Compact and brisk; always carrying a bag.

**Skin.** Light-medium brown, warm honey undertone, mid-tone `#9c6a45`; warm
highlights, never washed out.

**Face.** Heart-shaped, soft cheeks, a small cleft in the chin.

**Eyes.** Large, warm brown, tired but quick; they crease when she smiles.

**Brows.** Gently arched, medium thickness.

**Nose and mouth.** Small, upturned nose. Lips full with a coral lip balm; a quick,
bright smile.

**Hair.** **Natural hair in a high rounded puff with a navy-and-white scarf band**
across the front. Constant.

**Marks and details.** Small gold stud earrings; a nurse's fob watch pinned at her
chest; a thin gold chain.

**Signature outfit.** A **coral-pink short-sleeved tunic** over **navy trousers**,
white nursing shoes, a navy tote bag on her shoulder; a **lime-green phone case** (her
one accent).

**Props.** A phone in a lime case; a tote bag; a paper cup of water.

**Gestures.** Types with her thumb while walking; glances at the fob watch; presses a
hand to her chest in relief.

**Expressions.** *Default:* brisk and friendly. *Hurried:* a glance at her watch.
*Worried:* a small frown at the phone. *Relieved:* a hand on her chest and a smile.

**Voice.** Light, quick soprano; Lagos English with Yoruba warmth.

**Master prompt (copy exactly):**

> **Mrs Bimpe Lawal**, a 38-year-old **Nigerian woman**, **light-medium brown skin with
> a warm honey undertone**, **heart-shaped face, soft cheeks, a small cleft in the
> chin**, large warm brown tired-but-quick eyes, gently arched brows, coral lip balm, a
> quick bright smile; **natural hair in a high rounded puff with a navy-and-white
> scarf band across the front**; small gold stud earrings, a nurse's fob watch pinned
> at her chest; wearing a **coral-pink short-sleeved tunic, navy trousers, white
> nursing shoes**, a navy tote bag on her shoulder, **a phone in a lime-green case**;
> compact and brisk, friendly and hurried, a nurse and parent in Lagos

## Mr Segun Danjuma: a parent

**Who he is.** A parent at Bright Hill Academy: father of Dayo (Primary 4). Runs a small
building-materials shop in Ikorodu; takes cash all day and prefers to pay in cash
too, in person, with a receipt. Cheerful, a little loud, proud of his son.

**Age and build.** 43. 1.80 m (5'11"). Broad-shouldered and sturdy, a slight belly.

**Skin.** Dark warm brown with a reddish undertone, mid-tone `#6e4330`; warm
highlights, never ashy.

**Face.** Round, full cheeks, a wide jaw.

**Eyes.** Dark, round, merry; they squint when he laughs.

**Brows.** Thick, black, expressive.

**Nose and mouth.** Wide nose. A **full black beard trimmed short and even**. A big,
easy laugh.

**Hair.** Black hair cut short with a sharp, neat edge. Constant.

**Marks and details.** A thick silver chain bracelet on the right wrist, a plain
silver ring on the left hand, a pencil behind his right ear.

**Signature outfit.** A **short-sleeved sky-blue checked shirt** tucked into **dark
grey trousers**, a brown leather belt, brown loafers; a **lime-green pen** in the shirt
pocket (his one accent).

**Props.** A **thick brown envelope of cash**; a car key on a lime fob; a small notebook.

**Gestures.** Hands over the envelope with both hands and a nod; counts notes with
his thumb; laughs and slaps the counter lightly.

**Expressions.** *Default:* cheerful and open. *Counting:* serious, lips pressed.
*Proud:* chin up, a broad smile. *Reassured:* a deep nod.

**Voice.** Strong, warm baritone, quick to laugh; Lagos English.

**Master prompt (copy exactly):**

> **Mr Segun Danjuma**, a 43-year-old **Nigerian man**, **dark warm brown skin with a
> reddish undertone**, **round face, full cheeks, a wide jaw**, merry dark round eyes,
> thick black expressive brows, **a full black beard trimmed short and even**, a big
> easy laugh; **short black hair with a sharp neat edge**, a pencil behind his right
> ear, a thick silver chain bracelet on the right wrist; wearing a **short-sleeved
> sky-blue checked shirt tucked into dark grey trousers, a brown leather belt, brown
> loafers, a lime-green pen in the shirt pocket**, a thick brown envelope in his hand;
> broad-shouldered and sturdy, cheerful and proud, a shopkeeper and parent in Lagos

## The places

1. **Bright Hill Academy, Ikorodu**: a long two-storey classroom block painted cream with
   a pine-green stripe, a red-earth yard, a flame tree, a green iron gate with a small
   gatehouse and a folding stool, children's drawings along a corridor wall.
2. **The bursar's office**: a wooden desk under a ceiling fan, grey filing cabinets,
   green fee ledgers stacked, a window onto the yard, a rubber stamp, a ringing desk phone.
3. **The proprietor's office**: a bookshelf, a framed school crest, a term planner on a
   dark desk, a tray of tea, soft morning light.
4. **A clinic corridor in Ikorodu** (Mrs Lawal): pale green walls, a nurses' station, a clock.
5. **A building-materials shop, Ikorodu** (Mr Danjuma): stacked cement bags, paint tins,
   a wooden counter, a cash tin.
