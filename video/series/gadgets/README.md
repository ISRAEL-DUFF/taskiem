# Series 3: Phones and gadgets

"How Techline Gadgets runs on Taskiem, from the first phone on the shelf to a
dealer's big order." Part of [the series plan](../README.md). Draft for
approval, 18 November 2026.

Cut in two shapes, 9:16 and 16:9, each with an English and a Nigerian Pidgin

## 1. The business and the people (all invented, for approval)

**Techline Gadgets**, Computer Village, Ikeja, Lagos. (A working name: check
that no real shop uses it before we publish.) Phones, chargers, earbuds,
power banks and tablets. A busy counter, plus resellers across the country who
buy in bulk.

| Person | Role | Look and manner (to be designed) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ikenna Nwachukwu**, 33 | Owner. Fast talker, knows every model and every price by heart; a shrewd haggler who keeps his word | Needs a bible entry |
| **Maryam Sani**, 25 | Sales attendant. Careful with cash; learns the till and the chat in a week | Needs a bible entry |
| **Alhaji Ibrahim Gwarzo**, 58 | Distributor in Kano; Ikenna's supplier. Calm, formal, sells by the carton | Needs a bible entry |
| **Kehinde Alabi**, 36 | A reseller in Ibadan who buys phones in bulk to sell on | Needs a bible entry |
| **Emeka Nwosu** | The rider (existing) | In the bible |

None has a face or voice yet. Each new person needs a full entry in
`characters/bible.md` and Flow reference images before any clip is made.

**No real brands.** Products have invented names ("Nova X5", "Pulse 12",
"Volt power bank 20,000 mAh") so we never show or imply a real brand.

## 2. The story through the series

One week at Techline. A new phone range arrives from Kano; Ikenna puts it on
the shelf in storage sizes and colours, sells at the counter and on WhatsApp,
and restocks before he runs out. Kehinde, a reseller in Ibadan, wants twenty
phones: they haggle, agree a price under his lowest with his OK, and she pays
on credit terms. The week ends on his numbers.

## 3. What the app does for this business, and what it does not

**Shown (the app does it today):**
- Products with **sizes and colours as a grid with stock in each cell**, here
  storage (64, 128, 256 GB) by colour (Black, Blue, Gold).
- Photos from the camera, categories (Phones, Chargers).
- Selling at the counter and from a customer's WhatsApp order.
- **Price lists for trade buyers**, a **lower price from a quantity**, a **lowest
  price** staff cannot go below without the owner's OK; offers, counters,
  agreed prices kept for the buyer.
- **Days to pay and a credit limit** per trade buyer, checked where an order is
  accepted.
- **Restocking that drafts itself**: a low-stock card whose Reorder opens the
  order to a linked supplier.
- Invoices, matching a transfer, bills, approvals for money going out, the
  morning briefing (low stock told per variant), staff, tasks, shift handover
  with cash counted (short or over), riders for same-day delivery.

**Not in the app today, so not shown or implied:** serial or IMEI numbers per
device, warranty, repairs, trade-ins, instalment sales for customers. If gadget
shops need these, tell the product team; the series should not promise them.

## 4. Episodes

Lengths: vertical cut / YouTube cut. **Check** marks something to verify in the

### Episode 0: One week at Techline
- **Goal:** see the whole week; know what each episode teaches.
- **Length:** 45 to 60 s / 2 to 2:30. Made last, from the other episodes.
- **Opening:** Ikenna at the counter: three customers, two phones ringing.
- **Closing:** the map of episodes; **Start your free trial**.
- **Bridge:** "It starts with opening the shop."

### Episode 1: Open your shop
- **Goal:** signed up, in the Phones and gadgets category, findable.
- **Shows:** sign up, the business details, picking the category, appearing in
  Explore for a reseller.
- **Hero feature:** a reseller finds Techline in Explore.
- **Length:** 40 s / 1:15.
- **Bridge:** "The shop is open but the shelves are empty. What goes on them?"
  (Pidgin draft: "Shop don open but shelf dey empty. Wetin go dey inside?")
- **Next card:** Episode 2: Put your goods in.

### Episode 2: Put your goods in
- **Goal:** one phone as one product with every storage and colour.
- **Shows:** Add product, category, photos, "Make one product with sizes and
  colours" with Storage by Colour and a count in each cell.
- **Hero feature:** one product, not eighteen rows.
- **Length:** 60 s / 1:30.
- **Recap:** "Techline is open. Time to stock it." (Pidgin draft: "Shop don open.
  Make we stock am.")
- **Bridge:** "A customer messages on WhatsApp for a Nova X5. How does Ikenna take
  the order?"
- **Next card:** Episode 3: Take orders.

### Episode 3: Take orders
- **Goal:** a counter sale and a WhatsApp order, with the exact variant.
- **Shows:** Sell picking storage and colour; a WhatsApp order read into an
  order card, Confirm.
- **Hero feature:** Sell finds the right variant; stock comes down by one in that cell.
- **Length:** 70 s / 1:50. **Check:** does the order reader match a storage and

  card, and leave the WhatsApp order to the generic how-to.
- **Recap:** "Nova X5, 128 GB, blue."
- **Bridge:** "The order is in. How does he get paid?"
- **Next card:** Episode 4: Get paid.

### Episode 4: Get paid
- **Goal:** an invoice sent, a transfer matched.
- **Shows:** a company buys ten tablets: New invoice, sent by SMS with a pay
  link; the transfer that named no invoice matched to it in To match.
- **Hero feature:** matching a payment in one tap.
- **Length:** 55 s / 1:25.
- **Recap:** "A company wants ten tablets."
- **Bridge:** "Paid. Now the goods have to move."
- **Next card:** Episode 5: Deliver.

- **Shows:** the order needs a rider, Assign rider, Emeka's offer on WhatsApp,

- **Recap:** "The tablets are paid for."

- **Recap:** "Kehinde wants twenty."
- **Bridge:** "Orders, stock, money, a team. How does Ikenna see it all?"

- **Goal:** the morning briefing and "how did we do".

- **Length:** 50 s / 1:15.
- **Recap:** "Friday morning."
- **Bridge:** "Phones are valuable and so are the payments. How does he keep it safe?"

- **Length:** 50 s / 1:15. **Check:** the app lock is a phone-app feature; film it
