# Series 3: Phones and gadgets

"How Techline Gadgets runs on Taskiem, from the first phone on the shelf to a dealer's big
order." Part of [the series plan](../README.md). Drafted for approval 18 November 2026; updated
25 November 2026 to the seventeen films as rendered. The series is **the Food series retold for
a phone shop**: the same episodes and films in the same order
([`../food/README.md`](../food/README.md)), a new story.

Cut in two shapes, 9:16 and 16:9, each with an English and a Nigerian Pidgin narration. **Pidgin
lines are drafts for a native speaker to check.** English is the source. Where the footage differs
from the real app, read [`FIDELITY.md`](FIDELITY.md) before publishing; how the films were made is
in [`FILMING-NOTES.md`](FILMING-NOTES.md); the cast and their Flow prompts are in
[`characters.md`](characters.md).

plus resellers across the country who buy in bulk.

## 3. What the app does for this business, and what it does not

**Shown (the app does it today):**
- Products with **sizes and colours as a grid with a count in each cell**, here Storage by Colour.
- Photos from the camera, categories (Phones, Chargers).
- Selling at the counter picking storage and colour; a WhatsApp order read into an order card.
- **Price lists for trade buyers**, a **lower price from a quantity**, a **lowest price** staff
  cannot go below without the owner's OK; offers, counters, an agreed price.
- **Restocking that drafts itself**: a low-stock card whose Reorder opens the order to a linked
  supplier (the card names the variant).
- Invoices, matching a transfer, approvals for money going out, the morning briefing, staff, tasks,
  shift handover with cash counted (short or over), riders for same-day delivery, the app lock.

**Reshaped, because the app differs from the first outline** (details in `FIDELITY.md`):
- **The briefing shows only a count of low items**, so "low stock told per variant" is **not** shown
  in Episode 9. The variant is named on the low-stock card in Episode 7.
- **The WhatsApp order card is a stand-in** and the order reader matches a said size only when it
  equals the stored value, so the series says only that Taskiem reads the message into an order
  card.
- **Maryam is a Shift manager** (Handover is a Shift manager tool).
- **The deal and the price list are stood in** as in Food: the lowest price is not enforced by the
  stand-in, the card says "Lapses in 47 hours", the **credit limit and days to pay are not shown**
  (so not said), and the price-list film ends before the Save changes result.
- **Not shown, not implied:** serial or IMEI numbers per device, warranty, repairs, trade-ins,
  instalment sales for customers. If gadget shops need these, tell the product team.
