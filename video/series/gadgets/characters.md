# Phones and gadgets series: the cast

Four new people and one customer, all at or around **Techline Gadgets**, Computer Village,
Ikeja, Lagos. Emeka Nwosu (the rider) is already in `video/characters/bible.md`, section 9
(master prompt there; he wears the lime helmet and pine jacket here too). The customer
**Chiamaka Eze** reuses the design of Food's customer, Chioma Nnadi
([`../food/characters.md`](../food/characters.md)) under a new name, so her approved reference
sheets can be reused; see her entry below. All fictional; the bible's style, palette, skin
rules, global prompts and drift checklist (sections 2, 3 and 12) apply to everyone here. Each
master prompt is copied as it is into every generation, with the scene line after it and the
global negative prompt (for Flow: the "guard line" of `video/characters/flow-guide.md`
instead). **Draft for approval:** none of the four has an approved reference sheet yet; make the
sheets first (front, three-quarter, side, expressions) and keep them in
`video/characters/approved/<character>/`.

**No real brands.** No phone, bag or shirt shows a logo or a real brand; phone boxes are plain
white or grey. Do not generate text on boxes or screens.

## Ikenna Nwachukwu: the owner

**Who he is.** Owner of Techline Gadgets in Computer Village. Fast talker who knows every
model and every price by heart; a shrewd haggler who keeps his word. Busy all day, a phone in
each hand, proud that the shop has grown from one glass counter. The main teacher of the series.

**Age and build.** 33. 1.76 m (5'9"). Compact and quick, a little restless; moves in short
fast steps, leans on the counter when he talks.

**Skin.** Medium-deep brown with a warm reddish undertone, mid-tone `#6a4128`; luminous, warm
bronze highlights on the cheekbones, never grey.

**Face.** Square-ish, a strong jaw, full cheeks; expressive and mobile.

**Eyes.** Bright, dark, quick; they narrow when he names a price and widen when a deal lands.

**Brows.** Thick, black, one lifts higher than the other when he bargains.

**Nose and mouth.** Broad nose. Lips full; a fast, wide, confident grin; a **short neat
beard** along the jaw with a clean line.

**Hair.** Short black hair, a sharp **high-top fade** with a crisp part cut on the left.
Constant.

**Marks and details.** A **single wireless earbud in his left ear** (plain white, no logo),
a thick **steel watch** on the left wrist, a small **gold stud** in the right ear.

**Signature outfit.** A **charcoal-grey short-sleeved polo shirt**, **dark navy chinos**, **clean
white sneakers**; a **lime-green phone case** (his one accent).

**Props.** Two phones in plain cases, a calculator with a fat display, a stack of plain boxed
phones, a pen behind the ear.

**Gestures.** Taps the glass counter twice when he names a price; holds up two fingers to
mean "two thousand off"; talks with one hand while the other swipes his phone.

**Expressions.** *Default:* quick and friendly. *Haggling:* one brow high, a half-grin.
*Pleased:* a wide grin and a nod. *Concerned:* lips pressed, eyes on the phone.

**Voice.** Quick, bright tenor; fast Lagos English with a little Igbo cadence ("na so", "my
friend"). Slows down only when he says a price.

**Master prompt (copy exactly):**

> **Ikenna Nwachukwu**, a 33-year-old **Nigerian man**, **medium-deep brown skin with a warm
> reddish undertone**, **square face, a strong jaw, full cheeks**, bright quick dark eyes,
> thick black brows, a wide confident grin, **a short neat black beard**; **short black
> high-top fade with a crisp part on the left**, a **white wireless earbud in his left ear**,
> a small gold stud in the right ear, a steel watch on the left wrist; wearing a
> **charcoal-grey short-sleeved polo shirt, dark navy chinos, clean white sneakers**, a
> **lime-green phone case**; compact, quick and restless, a phone-shop owner in Lagos

## Maryam Sani: the sales attendant

**Who she is.** Sales attendant at Techline, 25. Careful with cash, polite with customers,
quiet until she is sure. Learns the till and the chat in a week; in the app she is a **shift
manager** (so she can hand the shift over). Ikenna trusts her with the till and she counts
twice.

**Age and build.** 25. 1.64 m (5'4"). Slim and upright; neat, economical movements.

**Skin.** Medium brown with a warm golden undertone, mid-tone `#8a5a3a`; warm highlights,
never ashy.

**Face.** Oval, soft cheekbones, a gentle chin.

**Eyes.** Large, dark, calm; they drop to the cash and come back up.

**Brows.** Neat, softly arched.

**Nose and mouth.** Small straight nose. Lips medium with a soft **rose-brown** lipstick; a
shy, closed smile that opens into a real one when she is proud.

**Hair.** Covered by a **pine-green hijab**, pinned neatly under the chin, a little of the
hairline showing. Constant.

**Marks and details.** Small **silver stud earrings**, a slim **silver watch** on the right
wrist, a **name badge on a short cord** (no readable text).

**Signature outfit.** A **cream long-sleeved tunic** to mid-thigh over **dark grey straight
trousers**, flat **black shoes**, the pine hijab; a **lime-green phone case** (her one accent).

**Props.** A phone in a lime case, a notepad with counted figures, a cash drawer, a stack of
charger boxes.

**Gestures.** Counts notes twice with her thumb; tucks the notepad under her arm; looks at
Ikenna before agreeing to a price below the list.

**Expressions.** *Default:* calm and attentive. *Counting:* lips move silently, brows
together. *Proud:* a quick open smile and a small nod. *Unsure:* eyes to Ikenna, a held breath.

**Voice.** Soft, clear mezzo, careful; Hausa-inflected Nigerian English. "Yes, sir." "Let me
count it again."

**Master prompt (copy exactly):**

> **Maryam Sani**, a 25-year-old **Nigerian woman**, **medium brown skin with a warm golden
> undertone**, **oval face, soft cheekbones, a gentle chin**, large calm dark eyes, softly
> arched brows, rose-brown lipstick, a shy closed-lip smile; **a pine-green hijab pinned
> neatly under the chin**; small silver stud earrings, a slim silver watch on the right wrist,
> a name badge on a short cord; wearing a **cream long-sleeved tunic over dark grey trousers,
> flat black shoes**, a **lime-green phone case**; slim and upright, careful and calm, a shop
> attendant in Lagos

## Alhaji Ibrahim Gwarzo: the distributor

**Who he is.** Distributor in Kano (Gwarzo Distributors) and Ikenna's supplier; sells phones
by the carton. Calm, formal, unhurried; a man of his word who sets terms and expects them
kept. Speaks softly and lets the other person finish.

**Age and build.** 58. 1.78 m (5'10"). Tall, broad-shouldered, a slow, dignified walk; a
slight stoop at the desk.

**Skin.** Deep brown with a warm, slightly bronze undertone, mid-tone `#5a3a26`; weathered,
luminous, never grey.

**Face.** Long, high forehead, deep lines at the eyes and mouth; a calm face.

**Eyes.** Dark, steady, heavy-lidded; they close briefly when he thinks.

**Brows.** Thick, black and grey.

**Nose and mouth.** Prominent straight nose. A **full grey-white beard**, neatly trimmed;
a slow, warm, closed smile.

**Hair.** Covered by a **white embroidered Kano cap (hula)**, a little grey at the sides.
Constant.

**Marks and details.** **Wire-rimmed reading glasses** on a thin cord, a **string of wooden
prayer beads** in his left hand (a small lime-green tassel is his one accent), a slim gold
watch.

**Signature outfit.** A **cream-white kaftan** (babban riga style, plain, with subtle
embroidery at the neckline), matching trousers, **brown leather slippers**.

**Props.** An old ledger with a worn spine, a phone with a leather flip case, stacked plain
cartons, a glass of tea.

**Gestures.** Slides the beads through his fingers while he listens; taps the ledger once
before agreeing; lays a hand on his chest when he promises.

**Expressions.** *Default:* calm and courteous. *Considering:* eyes closed a moment, a slow
nod. *Pleased:* a slow smile, a hand on the chest. *Firm:* a level gaze, the beads still.

**Voice.** Low, slow baritone; formal English with a Hausa lilt. "You are welcome." "It will
be done."

**Master prompt (copy exactly):**

> **Alhaji Ibrahim Gwarzo**, a 58-year-old **Nigerian man**, **deep brown skin with a warm
> bronze undertone**, **long face, high forehead, deep lines at the eyes and mouth**, steady
> dark heavy-lidded eyes, thick black-and-grey brows, **a full neatly trimmed grey-white
> beard**, a slow warm closed smile; **a white embroidered Kano cap**, wire-rimmed reading
> glasses on a thin cord, wooden prayer beads with a small lime-green tassel in his left hand,
> a slim gold watch; wearing a **cream-white kaftan with subtle neckline embroidery, matching
> trousers, brown leather slippers**; tall and broad-shouldered, calm and formal, a
> phone distributor in Kano

## Kehinde Alabi: the reseller

**Who she is.** A reseller in Ibadan, 36. Buys phones in bulk to sell on across the city and
the towns around it; a sharp, friendly negotiator who never raises her voice and always asks
for a little more. Pays on agreed terms.

**Age and build.** 36. 1.70 m (5'7"). Sturdy and confident; a straight back, a firm
walk.

**Skin.** Deep brown with a cool-warm undertone, mid-tone `#58361f`; warm bronze highlights.

**Face.** Round, high cheeks, a defined jaw; open and amused.

**Eyes.** Dark, bright, sharp; they flick to the price and back.

**Brows.** Strong, level, black.

**Nose and mouth.** Broad nose. Lips full with a **plum** lipstick; a slow, knowing smile.

**Hair.** **Short natural hair in a tapered cut**, black. Constant.

**Marks and details.** Large **gold hoop earrings**, a **gold bangle** on the right wrist,
a plain wedding band.

**Signature outfit.** A **deep plum structured jacket** over a **white round-neck tee**,
**black tailored trousers**, **tan leather loafers**; a **lime-green tote strap** (her one
accent) on a black tote.

**Props.** A phone with a black case, a black tote, a small notebook with figures, a pen.

**Gestures.** Taps the pen twice on the notebook before a counter; tilts her head with one
brow raised; spreads one hand as if to say "meet me halfway".

**Expressions.** *Default:* friendly and sharp. *Bargaining:* a half-smile, one brow up.
*Satisfied:* a slow grin and a nod. *Firm:* a level gaze, the pen still.

**Voice.** Confident contralto, smooth and unhurried; Ibadan Yoruba-inflected English
("my brother", "abeg").

**Master prompt (copy exactly):**

> **Kehinde Alabi**, a 36-year-old **Nigerian woman**, **deep brown skin with a cool-warm
> undertone**, **round face, high cheekbones, a defined jaw**, bright sharp dark eyes, strong
> level black brows, plum lipstick, a slow knowing smile; **short tapered natural black
> hair**; large gold hoop earrings, a gold bangle on the right wrist, a plain wedding band;
> wearing a **deep plum structured jacket over a white round-neck tee, black tailored
> trousers, tan leather loafers**, a black tote with a **lime-green strap**; sturdy and
> confident, friendly and sharp, a reseller in Ibadan

## Chiamaka Eze: the customer

**Who she is.** An office administrator in Ikeja who orders a phone from Techline on WhatsApp,
in her own words, in a hurry, for delivery to her flat on Allen Avenue. **She is Chioma Nnadi's
design from Food** (31, teal blouse and cream skirt, braids with a copper-gold thread, ID
lanyard): reuse Chioma's approved reference sheets, and **change only the name**. Her details,
gestures and expressions are in [`../food/characters.md`](../food/characters.md). Do not
generate the two series' customers in the same scene.

**Master prompt (copy exactly):**

> **Chiamaka Eze**, a 31-year-old **Nigerian woman**, **medium brown skin with a warm golden
> undertone**, **round face, full cheeks, a dimple on the left**, bright wide-set dark eyes,
> soft arched brows, berry-brown lip gloss, a wide laughing smile; **shoulder-length black
> braids in a loose half-up knot with a thin copper-gold braid thread at the front**; small
> silver hoop earrings, an ID lanyard in a pale-lime holder, a watch on her left wrist;

> bag on her shoulder; curvy and quick on her feet, friendly and hurried, an office worker in
> Lagos

## The places

1. **Techline Gadgets, Computer Village, Ikeja**: a narrow shop with a glass display counter,
   shelves of plain unbranded phone boxes, a wall of chargers and cables on hooks, a stool
   behind the counter, a roll-up shutter, warm bulbs (never cold fluorescent), a small
   generator box by the door, a busy lane outside with umbrella-shaded stalls.
2. **Gwarzo Distributors, Kano**: a wide warehouse office with stacked plain cartons, a
   wooden desk with an old ledger, a woven mat, a ceiling fan, a window onto a sandy yard,
   a glass of tea.
3. **Kehinde's shop, Ibadan**: a bright market-street shop with phones on a back-lit shelf,
   a counter with a cash box, a rusted corrugated roof outside, red earth in the street.
4. **Okoro & Sons Ltd office, Ikeja**: a plain office with grey desks, a water dispenser,
   a window onto a flyover.
5. **A flat on Allen Avenue, Ikeja** (Chiamaka): a small living room, a sofa, a work bag by
   the door, a bright window.
6. **A Lagos street** (bible, section 11): danfo buses, okada bikes, painted walls.
