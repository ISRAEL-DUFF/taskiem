# Taskiem for you series: the cast

Two new people (**Tobi Adewale** and **Mama Tobi**), one person who joins from the Food
series (**Chioma Nnadi**), and three businesses reused from `video/characters/bible.md`
(**Ada Obi**, **Bayo Adeyemi**, **Kola Adebayo**). All are fictional; the bible's style,
palette, skin rules, global prompts and drift checklist (sections 2, 3 and 12) apply to
everyone here. Each master prompt is copied as it is into every generation, with the scene
line after it and the global negative prompt (for Flow: the "guard line" of
`video/characters/flow-guide.md` instead). **Draft for approval:** Tobi and Mama Tobi have
no approved reference sheet yet; make the sheets first (front, three-quarter, side,
expressions) and keep them in `video/characters/approved/<character>/`.

**Positioning for every clip:** these people use Taskiem beside the chats they already
have. Never stage Taskiem as a replacement for WhatsApp or Telegram, and never show
calls (not in the app) or a store download (the app is not in the stores yet).

---

## Tobi Adewale: the customer

**Who he is.** 27. A young professional in Lagos (a junior analyst at a logistics
company in Yaba); phone-first, brisk, friendly. Orders lunch from Ada's Kitchen, gets a
haircut from Kola, plans Sunday lunch with the family, and is patient about teaching his
mother. The main face of the series: he does everything the viewer will do.

**Age and build.** 27. 1.79 m (5'10"). Lean and easy; walks with his phone in one hand.

**Skin.** Medium-deep brown with a warm neutral undertone, mid-tone `#6f4630`; warm
highlights on the cheekbones and nose bridge, never grey.

**Face.** Oval, a defined jaw, a slightly broad nose, a quick open smile.

**Eyes.** Dark, bright, a little amused; they glance down to the phone and back.

**Brows.** Straight, dark, expressive.

**Nose and mouth.** Medium nose. Lips full; a neat, very short moustache; an easy smile
that shows his top teeth.

**Hair.** Short black hair in a **low taper fade with a flat top**. Constant.

**Marks and details.** A small silver stud in the left ear; a plain black fitness watch
on the left wrist; **white earbuds in a rust-orange case** hanging from his bag strap
(his one accent).

**Signature outfit.** A **denim-blue overshirt, sleeves rolled, over a plain white
t-shirt**, charcoal chinos, clean white sneakers; a slim grey backpack.

**Props.** A phone with a navy case; the earbuds case; a paper cup of zobo.

**Gestures.** Thumbs the phone one-handed while walking; turns the screen towards someone
to show them; taps the screen twice with the pad of one finger.

**Expressions.** *Default:* relaxed and curious. *Hungry:* a half-grin, eyebrows up.
*Patient (with Mama):* a soft smile, the phone held between them. *Pleased:* a quiet nod.

**Voice.** Easy tenor, clear Lagos English with a light Yoruba rhythm.

**Master prompt (copy exactly):**

> **Tobi Adewale**, a 27-year-old **Nigerian man**, **medium-deep brown skin with a warm
> neutral undertone**, **oval face, defined jaw, a quick open smile**, bright dark amused
> eyes, straight dark brows, **a neat very short moustache**; **short black hair in a low
> taper fade with a flat top**; a small silver stud in the left ear, a plain black
> fitness watch on the left wrist, **white earbuds in a rust-orange case hanging from his
> bag strap**; wearing a **denim-blue overshirt with the sleeves rolled over a plain white
> t-shirt, charcoal chinos, clean white sneakers**, a slim grey backpack; lean and easy,
> relaxed and curious, a young professional in Lagos

## Mama Tobi: Tobi's mother

**Who she is.** 54. A retired primary-school teacher in Surulere; sharp, proud and
careful, **new to apps**: she holds the phone a little away from her face, reads every
word aloud, and is not afraid to ask twice. She is never foolish; the series shows her
learning one clear step at a time, with her son beside her. Called "Mama Tobi" by
neighbours, as many mothers are.

**Age and build.** 54. 1.62 m (5'4"). Sturdy and composed; unhurried.

**Skin.** Medium brown with a warm reddish undertone, mid-tone `#7a4a34`; fine smile lines,
warm highlights, never ashy.

**Face.** Round, soft cheeks, a gentle double chin; deep smile lines.

**Eyes.** Warm, dark, attentive; they narrow when she reads small print.

**Brows.** Soft, thin, a little arched.

**Nose and mouth.** Small, rounded nose. Lips medium with a plum lipstick; a wide proud smile.

**Hair.** Mostly covered by a **coral-pink gele head tie** tied in a low neat fold (her one
accent); the little showing at the temples is grey. Constant.

**Marks and details.** **Large round reading glasses on a beaded chain**, small gold
earrings, a gold wedding band, a wooden bangle.

**Signature outfit.** A **cream lace blouse with a deep-indigo adire wrapper skirt**,
flat brown sandals, a small woven handbag.

**Props.** A phone with a large-print screen in a floral case; a notebook where she writes
steps down; a plate of lunch.

**Gestures.** Holds the phone at arm's length, then brings it closer; taps with one finger,
slowly, then looks up to her son for a nod; laughs and says "ah-ah".

**Expressions.** *Default:* attentive and a little careful. *Reading:* glasses on, brows
together, lips moving. *Delighted:* a hand on her chest, a wide smile. *Firm:* a gentle
shake of the head, a raised finger.

**Voice.** Warm, steady alto; clear English with a Yoruba lilt and the odd Yoruba word.

**Master prompt (copy exactly):**

> **Mama Tobi**, a 54-year-old **Nigerian woman**, **medium brown skin with a warm
> reddish undertone**, **round face, soft cheeks, deep smile lines**, warm dark attentive
> eyes, soft thin arched brows, plum lipstick, a wide proud smile; **hair covered by a
> coral-pink gele head tie in a low neat fold**, a little grey at the temples; **large
> round reading glasses on a beaded chain**, small gold earrings, a gold wedding band, a
> wooden bangle; wearing a **cream lace blouse, a deep-indigo adire wrapper skirt, flat
> brown sandals**, a small woven handbag; sturdy and composed, careful and proud, a
> retired teacher in Lagos

## Chioma Nnadi: the customer

**Who she is.** 31. Office administrator in Yaba; orders lunch for herself and two
colleagues from Ada's Kitchen on WhatsApp, in her own words, often in a hurry. Friendly
and direct; pays in cash at the door when she remembers, by transfer when she does not.

**Age and build.** 31. 1.65 m (5'5"). Curvy, quick on her feet, always carrying a work bag.

**Skin.** Medium brown, warm golden undertone, mid-tone `#8f5c38`; warm highlights.

**Face.** Round, full cheeks, a soft jaw; a dimple on the left.

**Eyes.** Bright, dark, wide-set; they widen when she sees food.

**Brows.** Soft, arched, expressive.

**Nose and mouth.** Small rounded nose. Lips full with a berry-brown gloss; a wide
laughing smile.

**Hair.** **Shoulder-length braids with a loose half-up knot**, black with a thin
copper-gold braid thread at the front. Constant.

**Marks and details.** Small silver hoop earrings. A **lanyard with an ID card (not
readable) in a pale-lime holder**. A watch on the left wrist.

**Signature outfit.** A **teal short-sleeved blouse** with a **cream pencil skirt**,
flat tan shoes, a canvas work bag on her shoulder.

**Props.** A phone with a clear case; a lunch bag; a notepad with the colleagues' orders.

**Gestures.** Types with one hand while holding her bag; looks up at a clock; laughs and
says "abeg".

**Expressions.** *Default:* bright and quick. *Hungry:* eyes wide, brows up. *Hurried:* a
glance at her watch. *Pleased:* both hands on her cheeks.

**Voice.** Bright mezzo, warm, quick; Lagos English with a little Pidgin ("abeg", "no
wahala").

**Master prompt (copy exactly):**

> **Chioma Nnadi**, a 31-year-old **Nigerian woman**, **medium brown skin with a warm
> golden undertone**, **round face, full cheeks, a dimple on the left**, bright wide-set
> dark eyes, soft arched brows, berry-brown lip gloss, a wide laughing smile;
> **shoulder-length black braids in a loose half-up knot with a thin copper-gold braid
> thread at the front**; small silver hoop earrings, an ID lanyard in a pale-lime
> holder, a watch on her left wrist; wearing a **teal short-sleeved blouse, a cream
> pencil skirt, flat tan shoes**, a canvas work bag on her shoulder; curvy and quick on
> her feet, friendly and hurried, an office worker in Lagos

*In this series.* Chioma is the customer who already orders from Ada's Kitchen on
Taskiem and shows how it is done. Reused as in the Food series (`video/series/food/characters.md`).

cape and a plain black tee.

## The places

1. **A Lagos flat and a Yaba office** (Tobi's): a sofa, a desk with a laptop, a window on the street.
2. **Mama Tobi's front room, Surulere**: a floral sofa, a framed photo, a ceiling fan, afternoon light.
3. **Ada's Kitchen, Yaba** (bible, section 11): white tiles, steel counters, big pots, steam.
4. **A Lagos street** (bible): danfo buses, okada bikes, painted walls.
