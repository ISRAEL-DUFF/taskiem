# Series: Taskiem for you (customers and everyone)

"How to deal with a business on Taskiem as a customer: find it, ask it, order, pay,
follow the order, book, share, plan together, and keep it safe." Part of
[the series plan](../README.md). Outline of 19 November 2026; **episodes reshaped
24 November 2026 to match the phone app**. Based on decision 0068 and the specs
(`docs/spec/taskiem-for-everyone.md`, `people-chatting*.md`).

**Taskiem is not a replacement for WhatsApp or Telegram.** It sits beside them and
automates a business's work alongside its conversations, so the series is about the
business matter (find, ask, order, pay, follow), never chatting for its own sake.
Same pattern as the other series: Episode 0, then one episode per chapter, recap and
bridge lines (English and a Pidgin draft), a Next card, Flow character clips, 9:16 and 16:9.
**Pidgin lines are drafts for a native speaker to check.** English is the source.

**Do not claim:** end-to-end encryption (the app says chats are **not**; Episode 10 says
so), calls (planned), a download from the app stores (not yet), a browser chat without the
app, "near you" (Discover says Near me is not here yet).

## 4. Call to action (open question for the founder)

**Open question, the call to action.** The other series end on **Start your free trial**;
that is for businesses. Customers have no trial and the app is **not in the stores yet**, so
"Get Taskiem" would promise a download that does not exist. This series uses a neutral line
for now: **Talk to a business on Taskiem** (`series.json`, with "Find a business, ask, order
and pay, beside the chats you already have"). Change it to "Get Taskiem" only once the app
is available, and say where. The builder's package README still prints a trial sentence and
a "Zuri Atelier" line from its template; do not use those for this series.
