## The business and the day

**Ada's Kitchen**, Yaba, Lagos: home-cooked Nigerian food, lunch packs and party trays
for offices and events, cooked fresh every morning. This is the chat explainer's story
told in full: **one working day**, from the 8am card to the end-of-day close. The numbers
are the explainer's (`video/films/chat/script.md`).

## The story numbers (use these in every film)
- **Yesterday's sales** ₦486,500, up 12% on last Wednesday; owed to Ada ₦212,000; 3 items
  low; 4 deliveries today. A rice order: **"Rice, 25kg bag, is running low": Reorder 20
  from Bayo Rice Mills.** Tunde asks to pay Bayo **₦450,000** (a first payment to that
  account: Ada must approve).
- **Party pack, 10 plates**: storefront price ₦48,000 (was ₦42,000 on Ada's trade list,
  now **₦45,000**); from 10 packs **₦43,000**; **lowest price ₦41,500** (₦830,000 for 20).
- **The deal** (Rivers Foods, 20 party packs; Ibinabo George): list ₦860,000 at the
  10-pack price; **offer ₦800,000**; **Tunde counters ₦840,000**; **Rivers counters
  ₦820,000**; ₦820,000 is under Ada's lowest (₦830,000), so Tunde's Accept **waits for
  Ada's OK**; Ada agrees; kept for Rivers Foods 7 days.
- **An office lunch invoice**: Chinedu's office, **₦37,500**; the transfer arrives from
  "OKONKWO C" naming no invoice; matched to the invoice.
- **Chioma's order**: two jollof rice and a zobo, delivered, ₦13,600 cash at the door
  (the how-to's numbers); a rider (Emeka) takes it.
