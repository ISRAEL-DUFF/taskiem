## Chioma Nnadi: the customer

**Who she is.** 31. Office administrator in Yaba; orders lunch for herself and two
colleagues from Ada's Kitchen on WhatsApp, in her own words, often in a hurry. Friendly
and direct; pays in cash at the door when she remembers, by transfer when she does not.

**Age and build.** 31. 1.65 m (5'5"). Curvy, quick on her feet, always carrying a work bag.

**Skin.** Medium brown, warm golden undertone, mid-tone `#8f5c38`; warm highlights.

**Face.** Round, full cheeks, a soft jaw; a dimple on the left.

**Eyes.** Bright, dark, wide-set; they widen when she sees food.

**Brows.** Soft, arched, expressive.

**Nose and mouth.** Small rounded nose. Lips full with a berry-brown gloss; a wide
laughing smile.

**Hair.** **Shoulder-length braids with a loose half-up knot**, black with a thin
copper-gold braid thread at the front. Constant.

**Marks and details.** Small silver hoop earrings. A **lanyard with an ID card (not
readable) in a pale-lime holder**. A watch on the left wrist.

**Signature outfit.** A **teal short-sleeved blouse** with a **cream pencil skirt**,
flat tan shoes, a canvas work bag on her shoulder.

**Props.** A phone with a clear case; a lunch bag; a notepad with the colleagues' orders.

**Gestures.** Types with one hand while holding her bag; looks up at a clock; laughs and
says "abeg".

**Expressions.** *Default:* bright and quick. *Hungry:* eyes wide, brows up. *Hurried:* a
glance at her watch. *Pleased:* both hands on her cheeks.

**Voice.** Bright mezzo, warm, quick; Lagos English with a little Pidgin ("abeg", "no
wahala").
