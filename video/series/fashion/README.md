# Series 1: Fashion

"How Zuri Atelier runs on Taskiem, from the first day to a full week." Part of
[the series plan](../README.md). Draft for approval, 17 November 2026.

Cut in two shapes, 9:16 (Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp) and 16:9 (YouTube), each
with an English and a Nigerian Pidgin narration. **Pidgin lines below are
drafts and must be checked by a native speaker before recording.** The
English lines are the source.

## 1. The business and the people

**Zuri Atelier**, Surulere, Lagos. Made-to-measure clothing: Ankara, aso-oke
and lace, cut to measure; staff uniforms for kitchens and hotels; outfits for
every owambe. (This is the business already in the demo world, `seed.ts`: it
sells kitchen clogs, chef jackets and an Ankara kaftan in sizes and colours,
and Ada's Kitchen orders uniforms from it.)

| Person | Role | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Zainab Yusuf** | Owner and designer. Quick eye, strict about deadlines, proud of her cutting | In the demo world; **needs a bible entry** |
| **Dami** (Damilola Ojo), 22 | Apprentice and shop attendant; learns the till and the chat | **Proposed, new** |
| **Alhaja Sade** | Sells fabric at Balogun; Zainab's supplier | **Proposed, new** |
| **Nneka Eze** | A customer: an owambe outfit in a hurry | **Proposed, new** |
| **Ada Obi** | Orders staff uniforms for her kitchen (a link to the Food series) | Existing |
| **Emeka Nwosu** | The rider | Existing |

All fictional. None has a face or voice yet: each gets a full entry in
`characters/bible.md` and Flow reference images before any clip is made.

## 2. The story that runs through the series

One week at Zuri Atelier. Nneka needs an outfit by Saturday. Zainab puts her
goods in, takes the order, fits, delivers and gets paid; the week also brings
a big uniform order from Ada's Kitchen, a fabric run to Alhaja Sade, and the
shop's numbers on Friday. Every episode picks up where the last stopped.

## 3. Episodes

Lengths: vertical cut / YouTube cut. "Needs" lists what the demo world must
have. **Check** marks something to verify in the app before filming.

### Episode 0: Zuri Atelier in one week
- **Goal:** see the whole journey; know what each later episode teaches.
- **Length:** 45 to 60 s vertical / 2 to 2:30 YouTube.
- **Beats:** one per chapter (10 to 15 s each), each tagged "Episode N".
- **Opening:** Zainab with a tape measure, phone buzzing: "Six orders, one phone."
- **Closing:** the map of the ten episodes; **Start your free trial**.
- **Bridge:** "It starts with opening her shop." (to Episode 1)
- Made last, from the other episodes' footage.

### Episode 1: Open your shop
- **Goal:** a business signed up, findable, with the right categories.
- **Shows:** sign up (web or app), the business's name and details, choosing the
  Fashion category, being findable in Explore.
- **Hero feature:** being found: a customer or buyer searches Explore and sees
  Zuri Atelier.
- **Length:** 40 s / 60 to 75 s. **Check:** sign-up in the web as filmed.
- **Recap:** (none, first episode, opens on the title.)
- **Bridge:** "Her shop is open and empty. What does she put on the shelves?"
  (Pidgin draft: "Shop don open, but shelf dey empty. Wetin she go put?")
- **Next card:** Episode 2: Put your goods in.

### Episode 2: Put your goods in
- **Goal:** one product with sizes and colours, with photos, not thirty rows.
- **Shows:** Products tool, Add product, category (Dresses), photos (take a
  photo, from the phone, paste a link), "Sizes and colours: Set up" on the product (a size by colour grid with a count in each cell).
- **Hero feature:** sizes and colours with their own stock.
- **Length:** 60 s / 1:30. **Needs:** an Ankara dress with sizes (10, 12, 14, 16)
  and colours.
- **Recap:** "Zuri Atelier is open. Time to stock it." (Pidgin draft: "Shop don
  open. Make we stock am.")
- **Bridge:** "Nneka messages: she wants a dress for Saturday. How does Zainab
  take her order?" (Pidgin draft: "Nneka message: she want dress for Saturday.
  How Zainab go take her order?")
- **Next card:** Episode 3: Take orders.

### Episode 3: Take orders
- **Goal:** sell at the counter; turn a customer's WhatsApp message into an order;
  book a fitting.
- **Shows:** Sell in the chat's tools picking the exact size and colour; a
  customer's WhatsApp order read into an order card, Confirm; Services and
  Book for a fitting (a service with its time and price).
- **Hero feature:** an order from WhatsApp, and a booked fitting.
- **Length:** 75 s / 2:00. **Check:** how well the order reader matches a size
  and colour said in words ("size 14, green"); if it does not match them, film
  the counter sale and the booking and leave the WhatsApp order for the
  generic how-to.
- **Recap:** "Nneka wants her dress by Saturday."
- **Bridge:** "The order is in. But a customer who has not paid is not a sale.
  How does Zainab get paid?"
- **Next card:** Episode 4: Get paid.

### Episode 4: Get paid
- **Goal:** an invoice sent, a transfer matched.
- **Shows:** Invoices tool, New invoice (sent by SMS with a link to pay), Money's
  To match, matching a payment to the invoice; the chat's line that says Paid.
- **Hero feature:** a payment that names no invoice, matched in one tap.
- **Length:** 60 s / 1:30. **Check:** a deposit or part-payment for custom
  orders: show only if the app does it today.
- **Recap:** "Nneka's order is in. Time to be paid."
- **Bridge:** "Paid, and the dress is ready. Now it has to reach Nneka before
  Saturday."
- **Next card:** Episode 5: Deliver.

### Episode 5: Deliver
- **Goal:** send an order with a rider and follow it.
- **Shows:** the order needs a rider, Assign rider, the rider's offer on
  WhatsApp, picked up, arrived, cash received or delivered; the Deliveries page
  (**Check:** this is a page away from the chat; film the web version only
  if we decide to, or show the chat's line).
- **Hero feature:** Emeka delivers an outfit; Nneka gets a message when it
  leaves and when it arrives.
- **Length:** 60 s / 1:30.
- **Recap:** "The dress is ready and paid."
- **Bridge:** "A uniform order of fifty is coming from Ada's Kitchen. Zainab
  cannot do it alone."
- **Next card:** Episode 6: Your team.

### Episode 6: Your team
- **Goal:** add Dami, give tasks, clock in, hand over a shift.
- **Shows:** add by phone number from the Taskiem chat, Dami joins from
  WhatsApp, usual hours; give a task from Tasks
  (Give a task: "Finish 12 aprons by Friday"); Done from the chat; Clock in; Handover at closing.
- **Hero feature:** a task given from Tasks, done on Dami's phone.
- **Length:** 75 s / 2:00. **Check:** Staff and Tasks pages are pages away from
  the chat; use the chat's views.
- **Recap:** "A big order is coming. Zainab needs help."
- **Bridge:** "To make fifty uniforms she needs cloth. Where does she buy it?"
- **Next card:** Episode 7: Your suppliers.

### Episode 7: Your suppliers
- **Goal:** link with a fabric seller, order from her price list, haggle.
- **Shows:** Find a business, Connect, Alhaja Sade accepts; her price list
  shared; Zainab orders fabric; an offer and a counter; approval if it
  goes over a limit.
- **Hero feature:** haggling in the chat, as trade is done.
- **Length:** 75 s / 2:00. **Needs:** a fabric supplier business with a price list.
- **Recap:** "Zainab needs cloth for fifty uniforms."
- **Bridge:** "Now the other side: Ada's Kitchen wants to order from Zainab."
- **Next card:** Episode 8: Sell to businesses.

### Episode 8: Sell to businesses
- **Goal:** share a price list with a trade buyer and take their offer.
- **Shows:** a price list for Ada's Kitchen with a lower price from fifty, the
  lowest price Zainab will accept, Ada's offer, a counter, Zainab's OK for
  anything under her lowest price.
- **Hero feature:** a quantity price and a lowest price staff cannot go below
  without her OK.
- **Length:** 75 s / 2:00. (The demo world already has this: Ada's Kitchen orders
  clogs, 36 pairs in 7 sizes at the 50-pair price.)
- **Recap:** "Ada's Kitchen wants fifty uniforms."
- **Bridge:** "Orders, money, deliveries, a team. How does Zainab keep track of
  it all?"
- **Next card:** Episode 9: Know your numbers.

### Episode 9: Know your numbers
- **Goal:** read the morning briefing and ask "how did we do".
- **Shows:** the 8am briefing card: sales yesterday against last week, owed to
  you, low stock (told per size and colour), deliveries; Got it; asking how the
  week did.
- **Hero feature:** a low-stock alert for one size and colour; Reorder.
- **Length:** 50 s / 1:15.
- **Recap:** "Friday. Zainab opens the briefing."
- **Bridge:** "Money is going out too. How does she keep it safe?"
- **Next card:** Episode 10: Keep it safe.

### Episode 10: Keep it safe
- **Goal:** approvals for money going out, and the app lock.
- **Shows:** Dami asks to pay Alhaja Sade; the Needs your OK card; the first
  payment to this account warning; Approve with a comment; the app lock.
- **Hero feature:** nothing leaves without her OK.
- **Length:** 50 s / 1:15. **Check:** the app lock is in the phone app, not the
  web; film it on a real phone later.
- **Closing, not a bridge:** "That is Zuri Atelier. **Start your free trial.**"
- **End card:** the series map and the trial call to action.

5. **Flow clips:** 3 to 5 per episode (the first and last beat, reactions).
6. **Kit:** the Next card, chapter tags, a Fashion accent.

## 5. How it is delivered

One zip for the whole series (`../PACKAGE.md`): the footage, captions, cards,
music, scripts and Flow prompts for every episode, to be finished in the
owner's own editor. No second pass.

Order of work: the app data and footage for Episodes 1 to 10, then Episode 0
last from their footage; scripts and prompts as each episode lands; cards and
music; the zip.
