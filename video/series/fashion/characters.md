# Fashion series: the cast

Four people join the existing cast (Ada Obi, Emeka Nwosu: `video/characters/bible.md`).
All are fictional. The style, palette, skin rules, global prompts and drift
checklist of `video/characters/bible.md` (sections 2, 3 and 12) apply to everyone
here. Each master prompt is copied as it is into every generation, with the
scene line after it and the global negative prompt (for Flow: the "guard line"
of `video/characters/flow-guide.md` instead of a negative prompt).

Approved by the founder on 24 November 2026 (Zainab, Dami, Alhaja Sade, Nneka).

---

## Zainab Yusuf: the owner

**Who she is.** Owner and head tailor of **Zuri Atelier**, Surulere, Lagos:
made-to-measure Ankara, aso-oke and lace, staff uniforms for kitchens and
hotels, outfits for every owambe. Quick eye, strict about deadlines, proud of
her cutting; warm with customers, firm with fabric. A tape measure is part of
her hands. She trusts the chat to keep her orders straight so she can keep her
scissors moving. Never flustered for long.

**Age and build.** 38. 1.70 m (5'7"). Slender, upright, long arms; moves with
the economy of someone who has cut ten thousand garments.

**Skin.** Rich deep brown with a warm bronze undertone, mid-tone `#6b4128`;
luminous, warm gold highlights on the cheekbones, never grey.

**Face.** Heart-shaped, high cheekbones, a defined, slightly pointed chin, a
strong forehead.

**Eyes.** Large, dark, almond, focused; they narrow when she measures and
sparkle when she sees a finished piece.

**Brows.** Sharp, neatly arched, dark; the left lifts when she judges a hem.

**Nose and mouth.** Straight nose. Full lips with a deep plum-brown lipstick,
always. A quick, confident smile; a rare wide laugh.

**Hair.** Natural black hair in **neat medium box braids gathered into a high
bun**, a few braids falling at the temples. Constant.

**Marks and details.** A **yellow fabric tape measure draped around her neck**
(always). A **pincushion bracelet on her left wrist** (a small red cushion with
pins). Small silver hoops. Short clean nails, a faint chalk mark on the right
fingertips.

**Signature outfit.** A **sleeveless indigo-and-gold Ankara print top** (a bold
geometric pattern; no real brand's print), a **plain pine-green (`#0d3b2e`)
apron with two pockets**, black trousers, flat black shoes. The pine apron is
her accent colour (the one brand item) with a **small lime-green button** on the
pocket.
*Alternates:* **Owambe day:** a full gele in the Ankara print, the same apron
off, tape measure kept. **Evening:** apron off, a cream blouse.

**Props.** A phone in a pine-green case, tucked in the apron pocket; fabric
shears; a chalk pencil.

**Gestures.** Tilts her head to measure with her eyes; points with the chalk;
tucks the phone under her chin when both hands are busy; counts stitches aloud.

**Expressions.** *Working:* lips pressed, eyes narrowed. *Pleased:* a quick
closed-mouth smile and a nod. *Delight:* wide smile, eyes crinkle. *Worry:* brows
tilt up in the middle. *Firm:* chin up, one brow raised.

**Voice.** Warm alto, brisk, Lagos English with a Yoruba lilt; says "my dear"
and "no wahala".

**Master prompt (copy exactly):**

> **Zainab Yusuf**, a 38-year-old **Nigerian woman**, **rich deep brown skin
> with a warm bronze undertone**, **heart-shaped face, high cheekbones, defined
> slightly pointed chin**, large dark almond eyes, sharp neatly arched brows,
> deep plum-brown lipstick, quick confident smile; **natural black hair in
> medium box braids gathered into a high bun**; **a yellow fabric tape measure
> draped around her neck**, a small red pincushion bracelet on her left wrist,
> small silver hoops; wearing a **sleeveless indigo-and-gold Ankara print top
> under a pine-green apron with two pockets and a small lime-green button**,
> black trousers; slender upright build, brisk and exacting, a tailor in a busy
> Surulere workroom

## Dami (Damilola Ojo): the apprentice

**Who she is.** 22. Zainab's apprentice and shop attendant, six months in.
Learns the till, the chat and the sewing machine in the same week; careful,
quiet, quicker than she thinks. Proud that Zainab lets her take a customer's
order. Asks "please, ma, how do I…?" and never asks twice.

**Age and build.** 22. 1.62 m (5'4"). Petite, light on her feet, small steady
hands.

**Skin.** Medium-deep brown with a golden undertone, mid-tone `#8a5a37`;
smooth, warm, a soft glow.

**Face.** Round with a soft chin, full cheeks, youthful.

**Eyes.** Big, round, dark-brown and attentive; she looks at the screen, then
up for approval.

**Brows.** Soft, medium, straight; they rise when unsure.

**Nose and mouth.** Small rounded nose. Natural lips, no lipstick on work days;
a shy small smile that opens into a grin when pleased.

**Hair.** Black **short twist-out with a patterned headband** (a thin Ankara-print
band, indigo and gold, matching Zainab's cloth). Constant.

**Marks and details.** A **dimple in the right cheek**. A **tiny gold stud in
each ear**. A **white sewing-machine bobbin tied on a string round her wrist**
(a fidget habit). Short, clean nails.

**Signature outfit.** A **plain cream short-sleeved work shirt with a
lime-green name tag** pinned at the chest (no readable text; it is added in
editing if needed), a **pine-green apron like Zainab's** (the same one-brand
accent), **dark jeans**, **white canvas sneakers**.
*Alternates:* **Counter day:** the same, a small crossbody bag. **Closing:**
apron off, a pine cardigan over the shirt.

**Props.** A phone with a lime-green case; a note pad and pencil; a roll of
tape; a receipt printer on the counter.

**Gestures.** Taps the screen with one finger and checks twice; counts naira
notes with a thumb flick; says "yes, ma" and a small curtsey-nod; bites her lip
when she is reading carefully.

**Expressions.** *Default:* a shy half-smile. *Focus:* brows together, lips
pressed. *Proud:* a wide grin, dimple, eyes down. *Unsure:* brows up, glances
at the phone. *Relieved:* a slow breath out.

**Voice.** Light, soft soprano, a little hesitant; Lagos English with a Yoruba
lilt; "yes, ma", "please", "let me check".

**Master prompt (copy exactly):**

> **Dami**, a 22-year-old **Nigerian woman**, **medium-deep brown skin with a
> golden undertone**, **round face, soft chin, full cheeks, dimple in the right
> cheek**, big round dark-brown attentive eyes, soft straight brows, shy small
> smile; **short black twist-out hair with a thin indigo-and-gold Ankara-print
> headband**; tiny gold studs, a white sewing bobbin on a string round her
> wrist; wearing a **cream short-sleeved work shirt with a lime-green name
> tag, a pine-green apron, dark jeans, white canvas sneakers**; petite and light
> on her feet, careful and eager, a shop apprentice in Lagos

## Alhaja Sade: the fabric supplier

**Who she is.** 58. Sells fabric by the yard and the bale at **Balogun market**,
Lagos Island, from a shop she has held for thirty years. Formal, unhurried,
exact about prices, generous to those who pay on time. She says what a cloth
costs and does not move far; Zainab's oldest supplier. Warm underneath.

**Age and build.** 58. 1.60 m (5'3"). Compact, sturdy, steady; sits upright on
a stool among bales.

**Skin.** Medium-deep brown, warm reddish undertone, mid-tone `#7d4d2e`; soft
laugh lines, healthy and warm.

**Face.** Broad oval, full cheeks, a gentle double chin, a calm forehead.

**Eyes.** Dark, warm, heavy-lidded, alert; they miss nothing about a cloth or a
price.

**Brows.** Soft, grey-flecked, slightly raised; the right one lifts when she
quotes a price.

**Nose and mouth.** Broad, soft nose. Lips full, a muted brick-red lipstick.
Smiles rarely and warmly.

**Hair.** **Fully covered by a magenta-and-gold aso-oke gele tied in a tall,
neat fan.** (A different colour from Ada's marigold wrap: magenta `#a1245b`
with gold threads.) Constant.

**Marks and details.** **Rimless reading glasses on a gold chain** (worn on the
chain, not on her face, in most shots). Gold bangles on both wrists. A **gold
ring on her left hand**. A small mole on the left cheek.

**Signature outfit.** A **royal-blue lace blouse (iro and buba style) with gold
embroidery at the neckline** and a **matching wrapper**; clean, bright, formal.
*Alternates:* **Quiet day:** a plain wrapper and a cream blouse, the gele kept.

**Props.** A phone held at arm's length; a wooden yard-measure; a bale of
fabric with a handwritten paper tag (not readable); a small notebook.

**Gestures.** Runs fabric between her fingers; measures a yard along her arm;
taps the counter once for "that is the price"; nods slowly.

**Expressions.** *Default:* calm, level. *Quoting:* one brow up, chin lifted.
*Amused:* eyes crinkle, mouth closed, shoulders shake once. *Agreed:* a slow
warm smile.

**Voice.** Warm contralto, slow, formal; Yoruba-inflected English; "my daughter",
"that is the price".

**Master prompt (copy exactly):**

> **Alhaja Sade**, a 58-year-old **Nigerian woman**, **medium-deep brown skin
> with a warm reddish undertone**, **broad oval face, full cheeks, soft double
> chin, small mole on the left cheek**, warm dark heavy-lidded eyes, soft
> slightly raised grey-flecked brows, muted brick-red lipstick; **hair fully
> covered by a tall neat magenta-and-gold aso-oke gele**; **rimless reading
> glasses on a gold chain**, gold bangles on both wrists, a gold ring on her left
> hand; wearing a **royal-blue lace blouse with gold embroidery at the neckline
> and a matching wrapper**; compact sturdy build, calm and exact, a fabric
> seller at Balogun market in Lagos

## Nneka Eze: the customer

**Who she is.** 29. A marketing assistant with an owambe on Saturday and an
outfit she has not got. Bright, a little frantic, decisive; she messages
Zuri Atelier on a Monday and wants it by Friday. She is the viewer: she wants
the order to be easy.

**Age and build.** 29. 1.67 m (5'6"). Athletic, tall for her stride; fast
walker.

**Skin.** Medium brown, warm olive-gold undertone, mid-tone `#9c6b44`; fresh,
glowing.

**Face.** Oval, strong jaw, high forehead.

**Eyes.** Bright, dark, expressive; they widen when she is pleased and flick to
the clock.

**Brows.** Full, expressive, thick; they do a lot of her talking.

**Nose and mouth.** Slim nose. Lips medium-full, a coral-pink gloss. A wide, fast smile.

**Hair.** **Shoulder-length natural curls, loose, with a golden-brown rinse at
the ends.** Constant.

**Marks and details.** **Round gold hoop earrings** (larger than Zainab's).
A **thin gold chain with a small pendant**. A **beauty spot above the lip on
the right**. A smart-watch with a lime-green strap (the one brand item).

**Signature outfit.** A **fitted mustard-yellow blouse** with a **white linen
blazer**, **blue jeans**, **tan block-heel sandals**; a canvas tote on her
shoulder.
*Alternate:* **Fitting day:** the same without the blazer; a wrap skirt in the
Ankara print she chose.

**Props.** A phone with a clear case; the tote; a mood-board photo on her
phone (never legible).

**Gestures.** Types with both thumbs; looks up fast; holds up the phone to show
a picture; taps the table in a rhythm when she waits.

**Expressions.** *Default:* bright and quick. *Eager:* brows up, a leaning-in
smile. *Impatient:* a sideways glance at the watch. *Delighted:* both hands to
her cheeks.

**Voice.** Bright mezzo, quick, a laugh in it; Lagos English, a little Pidgin
for emphasis ("abeg", "for real").

**Master prompt (copy exactly):**

> **Nneka Eze**, a 29-year-old **Nigerian woman**, **medium brown skin with a
> warm olive-gold undertone**, **oval face, strong jaw, high forehead, a small
> beauty spot above the right of her lip**, bright dark expressive eyes, full
> thick brows, coral-pink lip gloss, a wide quick smile; **shoulder-length
> natural curls with a golden-brown rinse at the ends**; large round gold hoop
> earrings, a thin gold chain with a small pendant, a smart-watch with a
> lime-green strap; wearing a **fitted mustard-yellow blouse under a white linen
> blazer, blue jeans**, tan block-heel sandals; athletic build, bright and
> decisive, a young professional in Lagos

## The places (reused for every Flow clip)

1. **Zuri Atelier workroom, Surulere:** a bright room, a cutting table with
   bolts of Ankara in indigo, gold and green, two sewing machines, a rack of
   finished outfits in plastic covers, a mirror with a fitting platform, a
   window on a yellow-painted wall. The counter has a receipt printer.
2. **Balogun market stall:** a narrow stall stacked floor to ceiling with bolts
   of fabric, a hanging scale, a stool, strong natural light from the doorway.
3. **Nneka's flat or office:** neat, modern, a sofa, a laptop, a mood board.
4. **A Lagos street:** yellow danfo buses, okada bikes, painted walls, mid-day.
